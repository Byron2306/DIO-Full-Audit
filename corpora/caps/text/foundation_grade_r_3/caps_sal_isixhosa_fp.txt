1.3 IINJONGO EZIPHANGALELEYO ZEKHARITYHULAM YOMZANTSI AFRIKA

(a) INkcazelo yePolisi yeKharityhulam nokuHlola yeBanga R-12 inika isikhokelo kulwazi, izakhono

(b) nezithethe (izinto ezixabisekileyo) ezinokufundwa ezikolweni zaseMzantsi Afrika. Le kharityhulam ijonge
 ukuqinisekisa ukuba abafundi bafumana ulwazi bekwasebenzisa nezakhono ngeendlela ezihambelana
 nobomi babo bemihla ngemihla. Kungoko ke le kharityhulam ikhuthaza uluvo lokusebenzisa kakhulu iimeko
 zalapha, kodwa ibe ikwayise iso nemiba ebalulekileyo yehlabathi jikelele.

(c) INkcazelo yePolisi yeKharityhulam nokuHlola yeBanga R-12 yenza oku kulandelayo:

 • Ixhobisa abafundi ngolwazi, izakhono nezithethe eziya kubanceda ukuba bakwazi ukuzimela
 nokuthabatha inxaxheba ekuhlaleni njengabemi belizwe elikhululekileyo; nokuba bavela kweyiphi imo
 yezentlalo, uhlanga, isini, bekhangeleka njani ngokomzimba okanye bekuliphi inqanaba lengqiqo;

 • Ivulela abafundi amathuba okuya kwimfundo ephakamileyo;

 • Incedisa abafundi ukuba bakwazi ukuphuma kumaziko emfundo baye kumaziko omsebenzi; yaye

 • Inika abaqeshi umfanekiso ocacileyo ngobuchule nolwazi abanalo abafundi.

(c) INkcazelo yePolisi yeKharityhulam nokuHlola yeBanga R-12 isekelwe phezu kwale mithetho-siseko ilandelayo:

 • Ukuphuculwa kwezentlalo: ukuqinisekisa ukuba kuyalungiswa ukungalingani kwezemfundo
 okwabangelwa lixesha lobandlululo, nokuba bonke abemi banikwa amathuba emfundo alinganayo;

 • Ukufunda ngokubandakanyeka kusetyenziswa ingqiqo: ukukhuthaza indlela yokufundisa efuna
 abafundi ukuba bathathe inxaxheba bebonakalisa ingqiqo, endaweni yokufunda ngokubethelela izimvo
 nokufunda nje bengaziqiqisisi iinyaniso okanye izimvo abazinikwayo;

 • Ulwazi nezakhono ezikumgangatho ophakamileyo: ukubekwa kweyona migangatho isezantsi
 yolwazi nezakhono ekufuneka abafundi bezibonakalise kwibanga ngalinye, kananjalo nokumiselwa
 kwemigangatho ephezulu enokufikelelwa kuzo zonke izifundo;

 • Ukuqhubela phambili: umxholo wokufundwayo neemeko ekufundwa phantsi kwazo kwibanga ngalinye
 ubonisa ukuqhubela phambili, ukususela kokulula ukuya koko kuntsonkothileyo;

 • Amalungelo oluntu, uqukaniso, ukukhathalelwa kokusingqongileyo nobulungisa bezentlalo: ukufakwa
 kwemithetho-siseko neendlela zokwenza ubulungisa kwezentlalo nokukhathalela indalo esingqongileyo
 kwanamalungelo oluntu, njengoko ebekiwe kuMgaqo-siseko weRiphablikhi yoMzantsi Afrika. INkcazelo
 yePolisi yeKharityhulam nokuHlola yeBanga 10-12 ibonakalisa uvakalelo kwimiba yeyantlukwano
 enjengendlala, ukungalingani, uhlanga, isini, ulwimi, ubudala, ukuba nenkubazeko neminye imiba;

2.1 IILWIMI KWINKCAZELO YEPOLISI YEKHARITYHULAM NOKUHLOLA

Ulwimi sisixhobo sokucinga nokunxibelelana. Lukwayindlela yenkcubeko nothando lobuhle abathi abantu babelane
ngayo ukuze bakwazi ukuliqonda ilizwe abaphila kulo. Ukufunda ukusebenzisa ulwimi ngendlela eyiyo kubangela
ukuba abafundi bafumane ulwazi, bakwazi ukuzazi, ukwazisa iimvakalelo neengcinga zabo, ukunxibelelana nabanye
nokukwazi ukulawula ilizwe abaphila kulo. Lukwanika abafundi imifanekiso etyebileyo nemandla kunye neengcinga
ezinokusetyenziswa kwilizwe labo ngokungcono nangokucacileyo kunokuba kunjalo. Kusetyenziswa ulwimi
ukuvakalisa nokwakha iinkcubeko ezizintlobo ngeentlobo nobuhlobo kwaye kukwangokusebenzisa ulwimi apho olu
lwakhiwo lunokuthi lutshintsha tshintshwe, lwandiswe okanye luphuculwe.

2.1.1 Imigangatho yolwimi

iTshivenda, iXitsonga nezi zingezizo ezaseMzantsi Afrika, ezizezi, Arabic, French, German, Gujarati, Hebrew,
Hindu, Italian, Latin, Morden Greek, Portuguese, Spanish, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu. Zonke iiLwimi zaseburhulumenteni
zinokufundiswa njengoLwimi lwaseKhaya, olokuQala oloNgezeleweyo okanye olwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo. Zonke
iilwimi ezingezizo ezaseMzantsi Afrika zinokufundwa ubuncinane kwinqanaba loLwimi lwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo.

Ukufunda ulwimi kumabanga 4-9 kuquka zonke iilwimi zaseburhulumenteni eMzantsi Afrika. Zonke ezilwimi
zisemthethweni. Zonke iilwimi ezisemthethweni zingafundiswa kumgangatho woLwimi lwaseKhaya uLwimi Lokuqala
okanye kumgangatho woLwimi Lwesibini Olongezelelweyo. Zonke iillwimi ezingezizo ezaseburhulumenteni
zifundiswa kumgangatho woLwimi Lwesibini Olongezelelweyo apho ezi lwimi ziphuhliswe khona.

ULwimi lwaseKhaya lulwimi lokuqala oluthi lufundwe ngabafundi ngelixa ulwimi Lokuqala Olongezelelweyo lufundwa
njengolwimi olongezelelwe kulwimi Lwasekhaya. ULwimi Lwesibini olongezelelweyo lolunye ulwimi olunokufundwa
ngumfundi, ulwimi oluthi luvumele unxibelelwano olubanzi nelwimi ezininzi njengoko oku kubhaliwe kuMthetho
Sisekelo neLanguage in Education Policy. Ulwimi lungasetyenziswa kwindawo ethile kwaye lwangeze ekwakheni
isizwe nokuqondana phakathi kwekcubeko. Izikolo ezininzi eMzantsi Afrika azilufundisi ulwimi lwaseKhaya lwabafundi
abathile okanye lwabafundi bonke abafunda kweso sikolo. Ezi zikolo zikhetha ukufundisa ulwimi olunye okanye
iilwimi ezimbini ezikwinqanaba lolwimi lwaseKhaya. Ngenxa yoku ileybheli uLwimi lwaseKhaya nolwimi Lokuqala
Olongezelelweyo lubhekiselele kwinqanaba lolwazi lololwimi olo luthi lufundiswe hayi ngokubhekiselele kulwimi
lokuzalwa (ulwimi lwaseKhaya) okanye ulwimi olufundwayo(njengolwimi olongezelelweyo). Ngokweenjongo zale
polisi konke okubhekiselele kuLwimi lwaseKhaya makuqondwe njengokubhekiselele kumgangatho hayi ulwimi.

Umgangatho woLwimi lwaseKhaya unika ulwazi lolwimi olubonisa izakhono zonxibelelwano olusisiseko nabanye

ekufundeni ikharityhulam. Ugxininiso kulo mgangatho wolwimi lusekufundiseni izakhono zokuphulaphula, ukufunda
nokubhala. Lo mgangatho ukwanika abafundi ulwazi loncwadi, ukubonisa uthando lobuhle nokukwazi ukuzakhela
umfanekiso ngqondweni. Oku kunika abafundi ithuba lokuyila ngokutsha, ukuyila engqondweni ilizwe abaphila kulo.
Nangona kunjalo ugxininiso nexesha elabelwe ukuphula phula nokuthetha kwibanga lesi-7 ukuya phambili liba

abafundi balungiselelwa imfundo ephakamileyo naxa beqalisa ukusebenza.

2.1.2 Injongo zokufunda uLwimi lwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo

 2.1.2.1 Injongo (eziphangaleleyo)

 Ukukhuthaza iilwimi ezongezelelweyo

 Indlela yokufundisa ebandakanya iilwimi zonke sisxhobo esibalulekileyo sokudibanisa uluntu, kunye nokukhula
 komntu ngamnye noluntu luphela. UMzantsi Afrika lilizwe elibandakanya zonke iilwimi ezisemthethweni
 ezilishumi elinanye. Kubalulekile ke ngoko, ukuba abantwana bafunde iilwimi ezongezelelweyo kwamsinya

 Iilwimi ezongezelelweyo zifundeka ngcono kakhulu xa abantwana benesiseko esomeleleyo kuLwimi lwabo
 lwaseKhaya (HL). ULwimi lwaseKhaya luba sisiseko sokufunda iilwimi ezongezelelweyo kwimeko ebandakanya
 iilwimi zonke.

2.1.3 Ukufundiswa koLwimi lwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo

ukwenzela ukwakha ulwazi lokusebenzisa ulwimi kunye nolwazi olusisiseko kweetekisi ngeetekisi. Ootitshala

nokuqonda (umz. Ingxoxo yababini), ukuzonwabisa (umz. ibali okanye ingoma). Ngaphezulu kwako konke, bafuna

okhaphayo), ulonwabo (umz. imagazini) uncwadi oluncomayo (umz. umbongo). Uphando luveza ukuba eyona
ndlela ibalulekileyo yokwakha isigama kukufunda ngamandla iitekisi ezifanelekileyo. Kubalulekile ukuba iitekisi
zomlomo/zeorali, ezibhalwayo nezibonwayo zibe kumgangatho olungele bafundi. Ukuba iitekisi zinzima kakhulu,
abafundi abayi kukhuthazeka kwaye abayi kufunda nto; kanti naxa zilula kakhulu aziyi kubacela mngeni kwaye
kuya kuba kuncinci okufundwayo. Indima ebalulekileyo katitshala wolwimi kukulungelelanisa umgangatho wetekisi
nomgangatho womfundi. KwisiGaba esiPhakathi siphela, abafundi mabaphulaphule ze bafunde iitekisi ezinobunzima
obuya bukhula ngokukhula, ezihambelana nokukhulisa izakhono zolwimi zabo.

Ugqaliso loLwimi lwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo (SAL) lukufundiso lolwimi ngokunxibelelana nophuhliso lwezakhono
ezisisiseko zokunxibelelana nabantu (BICS). Ugqaliso ikakhulu luya kuba kwimisebenzi yokuPhulaphula

ngo ‘lwimi olongezelelweyo’ elusa kubafundi bakhe abancinane. Indlela elungileyo yokwenza oku kukusebenzisa
iphaphethi, enokunikwa igama ngoLwimi oloNgezelelweyo, umzekelo, Iphaphethi uPule. Kwisifundo sokuqala,
utitshala wazisa iphaphethi aze axelele abafundi ukuba uPule akakwazi ukuthetha ulwimi lwabo lwasekhaya;
angathetha kuphela umz. iSetswana, ngoko ke baza kuthetha iSetswana noPule. UPule ngoko uza kusetyenziswa
rhoqo kwiklasi yoLwimi lwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo (SAL). Utitshala unokwazisa iphaphethi yesibini (umz. uPalesa

2.1.9 Ulawulo eklasini kunye nezixhobo zokufunda nokufundisa

Iiklasi zaseMzantsi Afrika ziziintlobo-ntlobo yaye zinobandakanyo. Kuqhelekile ukufumana abafundi abathetha
ngaphezulu koLwimi lwaseKhaya olunye olusenokungabi luLwimi lokuFunda nokuFundasa (LoLT) lwesikolo.
Kwezinye iimeko ulwimi olufundiswa njengelwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo (SAL) lusenokuba luLwimi lwaseKhaya
lwabanye abafundi. Kwiimeko ezinjalo utitshala wolwimi lwesibini olongezelweyo kuya kufuneka acwangcise
imisebenzi ukuhlangabezana nabathetha uLwimi lwaseKhaya (HL) nabathetha olwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo (SAL).
Ootitshala mabasebenzise abathetha ulwimi lwasekhaya njengomzekelo aze abaxhase ukuba bakhokele ngamaxesha
okufundisana bebodwa apho banokuxhasa abafundi boLwimi lwesiBini oloNgezelelweyo (SAL) (abangaluthethiyo).

Ukusetyenziswa koLwimi lwaseKhaya makusetyenziswe okokuqala ukuxhasa ukufundiswa koLwimi lwesiBini
oloNgezelelweyo (SAL). Amagama eludongeni anokwenziwa ukukhumbuza utitshala nabafundi isigama
esifundisiweyo kwiilwimi zontathu ezahlukeneyo ezifundisiweyo. Ootitshala bayakhuthazwa ukusebenzisa imibala
ukwahlula phakathi kwesigama esiphambili soLwimi lwaseKhaya, uLwimi lokuQala oloNgezelelweyo kunye
nolwesiBini i oloNgezelelweyo. Imidlalo yolwimi enjengo (Ndifak’ingalo yam, ndiphinde ndiyikhuphe….; Intloko,
amagxa, sifuba nengalo, sifuba nengalo….) ma isetyenziswe ukufundisa amabinzana olwimi nesigama esiphambili.
Izixhobo zokufunda nokufundisa ezinjenge (izinxibo, iiphowusta zengxoxo, IiNcwadi eziNkulu, iNgqokelela yamabali,
iingoma nemibongo, iimaski, iiphaphethi kuquka iiNcwadi eziNkulu ezenziwe ngutitshala nabafundi, iincwadi ezigoso-
goso (zig-zag) mazisetyenziswe.

ULUHLU LWAMAGAMA

ukuhlola (assessment) - inkqubo ecwangcisiweyo neqhubekayo yokuqokelela ulwazi malunga nokusebenza
komfundi, nokuba yenziwa ngokusesikweni okanye ngokungekho sesikweni.

ukuhlola okusisiseko (baseline assessment) - ukuhlola kokuqala okwenzelwa ukufumana ukuba sele esazi ntoni
umfundi. UkuHlola koNyaka kukaZwelonke (i-Annual National Assessment - ANA) kuya kwenziwa ekuqaleni kwekota
yokuqala kuwo onke amabanga yaye iya kuba kukuhlola okusisiseko.

ukuhlola okuqhubekayo (continuous assessment) - ukuhlola okuyinxalenye yokufundisa nokukhuliswa
kwabafundi ngokusoloko benikwa ingxelo ngomsebenzi wabo.

ukubhala ngokudibanisa (cursive writing) - indlela yokubhala ngesandla eyenziwa ngokubhala amagama
ungakhange uluphakamise usiba (pen) okanye ipensile. Kuya kuphuma itekisi edibeneyo enoonobumba abenzeke
kuloo ndawo luphakanyiswe kuyo usiba okanye ipensile.

ilitheresi ekhulayo (emergent literacy) - luthetha ulwazi lwabafundi olukhulayo malunga namagama. Abafundi
babona amagama abhaliweyo baze bawaqonde ukuba athetha ukuthini. Bafunda ngeencwadi namabali befundelwa
ngabantu abadala okanye abafundi abadala kunabo. Basenokuqala ukuzama ukubhala amagama abo besebenzisa
iindlela abacinga ukuba apelwa ngazo (upelo oluyiliweyo okanye upelo olukhulayo) yaye basenokuqala ukulinganisa
ukufunda iincwadi. Zonke ezi zenzo zibonisa ukukhula kolwazi lwabo lwelitheresi - ilitheresi ekhulayo.

itekisi ezotyiweyo (graphic text) - itekisi apho ulwazi lungohlobo olubonwayo umz. imizobo, iigrafu, njalo-njalo.

ukufunda okuncediswayo (guided reading) - lithuba lokufunda kweqela apho abafundi bakwinqanaba elinye
lokufundiswa aze utitshala ababonise indlela yokufunda ukwenzela ukuba bona baye bengenelela befunda ngokwabo.

ukubhala okuncediswayo (guided writing) - kulapho umfundi ngamnye okanye amaqela amancinci abafundi
abhala uluhlu lweentlobo zeetekisi emva kokuba utitshala ebanike isikhokelwana malunga nokubhala umz. ifomathi,
ukuphawulwa kwentetho (ngeekhoma, izingxi, njalo-njalo), iukusetyenziswa kolwimi okanye upelo.

ukuprinta okudityaniswayo (joint script) - luhlobo lokudibanisa olusekelwe kuhlobo oluthile lwendlela yokubhala
(script) engadibanisiyo, olwenzeka ngokuba amagama amaninzi adityaniswe ngenjongo yokukhawulezisa ukubhala.

isandi (phoneme) - izandi ezizimeleyo zolwimi (umz. ‘t’, ‘th’, ‘ty’, ‘dy’) Igama u-‘apha’ lineefonimi ezintathu ezizezi:
‘a’ ‘ph’ ‘a’.

ulwazi lwezandi (phonemic awareness) - ukukwazi ukwahlula izandi ezahlukeneyo zolwimi.

izandi zopelo (phonics) - ubudlelwane phakathi kwezandi nendlela ezipelwa ngayo. Izandi zopelo zisetyenziswa
ekufundeni nasekubhaleni.

ulwazi lwezandi (phonological awareness) - ukukwazi ukuqwalasela izandi zolwimi uzahlula kwintsingiselo yazo.

isingqi (rhyme) - amagama okanye imiqolo yombongo ephela ngesandi esinye (umz. hlamba, hamba, thamba).

ukufunda notitshala (shared reading) - umsebenzi apho abafundi bafunda kunye notitshala kwitekisi eyandisiweyo
yaba nkulu. Esi sisifundo seklasi yonke. Le tekisi isetyenziswayo ijoliswe kwelona qela liqhuba kakuhle apha eklasini.
Abanye abafundi baya kuphulaphula, abanye baqale kancinci ukufunda ngelixa abaninzi beya kube befunda
ngokupheleleyo. Le tekisi inye isetyenziswa iintsuku ezininzi. Utitshala ujonga indawo ekuza kugxilwa kuyo ngosuku
ngalunye. Itekisi le isetyenziselwa ukwazisa ngokusemxholweni iimpawu zetekisi, izandi, iukusetyenziswa kolwimi
nezakhono zokufunda.

amagama abonakala lula (sight words) - ngamagama umntu ofundayo awaqonda ngokuzenzekelayo (xa
ewabona). Akukho mfuneko yokuba ade acazululwe. Xa esafundiswa ukufunda umfundi amagama aqondeka lula
adla ngokuxhaphaka okanye amagama ‘asetyenziswa kakhulu’ afana nala: ‘umama’ ‘usisi’.
