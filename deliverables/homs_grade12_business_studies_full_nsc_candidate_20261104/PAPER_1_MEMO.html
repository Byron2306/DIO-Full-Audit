PartExpected evidence and allocationMarks
2.12 marks: threat is an external condition with adverse effect; 2 marks: opportunity is an external condition with potential benefit. Internal strengths/weaknesses are not threats/opportunities. Credit precise use of case evidence where requested; do not award unsupported assertions.4
