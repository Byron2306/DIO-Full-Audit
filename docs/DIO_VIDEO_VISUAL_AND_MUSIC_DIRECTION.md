# DIO video visual and music direction

## Reference language

The public DIO Workflows channel at `https://www.youtube.com/@DIOworkflows` uses a
recognisable near-black, graphite and warm-gold product identity. The current
product thumbnails use orbital, metallic and architectural material, then vary
the focal asset for the product: Vesper's market panel, VAMP's review snapshot,
EVIDEX's evidence trail, Sophia's research identity and HOMS's academic imagery.
Keep that common DIO signature while rotating verified backgrounds and topic
assets scene by scene. Avoid one repeated background across a whole video.

The house profile's scene map uses hash-verified assets from
`media/product_asset_packs/homs_corpo_cult_v1/pack.json`. The deterministic media
incarnation composes those assets into scene cards and records source asset IDs,
hashes and manifest hash in its media receipt. These are DIO-owned promotional
assets; they never replace customer or curriculum evidence. HOMS learner
explainers retain legible subject diagrams, maps, data tables and source material
where those teach the concept better than a cinematic plate.

## Music rotation

`config/media/music_catalog.json` is the pinned local catalog. Each selectable
track must have a verified audio hash, a separate rights receipt, and a separate
attribution file. The current choices intentionally cover distinct moods:

- Warm Sunset by MusicLFiles — supportive, calm, documentary and learning content.
- Soft Corporate by MusicLFiles — bright, human, acoustic and customer-launch work.
- Epic Trailer by Rafael Krux — cinematic, high-stakes product reveal and proof.

The marketing projection scores catalog metadata against the campaign family,
offer and audience, then uses a stable tie-break so a rerun remains reproducible.
Individual productions can pin a specific track. The selected rights and
attribution receipts travel with the output. Publication and music approval
remain human decisions.

The two newly added tracks are listed by their original Wikimedia Commons pages:
[Soft Corporate by MusicLFiles](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Soft_Corporate_by_MusicLFiles.ogg)
and [Epic Trailer by Rafael Krux](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Epic_Trailer_by_Rafael_Krux.ogg).
Both pages state CC BY 4.0, permitting commercial sharing and adaptation with
attribution and a licence link. Their local rights receipts preserve the source
page revision and match the downloaded file's published SHA-1 against the local
bytes. The pre-existing [Warm Sunset by MusicLFiles](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Warm_Sunset_by_MusicLFiles.ogg)
remains in the catalog for the warmer learning/documentary register.

## Review before release

- Check the exported frame sequence for contrast, product-specific visual fit,
  icon placement and readable captions.
- Check Vera narration remains intelligible above the selected bed.
- Include the relevant attribution and rights receipt with every derivative.
- Keep source/curriculum visuals attached to their own provenance; never use a
  decorative golden asset as evidence.
- Keep generated media in review state until the named owner clears it.
