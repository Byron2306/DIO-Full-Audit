# Educator scoring rubric

Score each injected defect from 0 to 3. The reference disposition is a benchmark
anchor, not a substitute for subject-teacher judgement.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Missed the defect or accepted an invalid item/memo. |
| 1 | Noticed a concern but could not identify its consequence or correct it. |
| 2 | Identified the primary defect and proposed a substantially sound correction. |
| 3 | Correctly diagnosed all material issues, corrected the artifact, checked downstream mark totals and recorded rationale. |

## Review dimensions

1. **Curriculum and coverage:** Grade-specific content is mapped to current CAPS
   and exam guidance; assess both breadth and depth, and record omissions.
2. **Item validity:** One defensible task is asked; wording is unambiguous;
   supplied sources contain the facts needed; marks match response demand.
3. **Memo correctness:** Recalculate numerical answers independently; reconcile
   item, section and paper totals; state accepted alternatives and mark rules.
4. **Cognitive demand:** Classify the work learners must do, not just the command
   verb or label. Record evidence for each classification.
5. **Language and differentiation:** Check grade-appropriate vocabulary, reading
   load, accessibility, and whether barriers are unrelated to the construct.
6. **Classroom usability:** Confirm instructions, time, materials, layout,
   marking consistency and accommodations are practical for a teacher to use.

## Decision thresholds

- Any critical memo or mark-total error: **fail and revise**.
- Any ambiguous, contradictory, unanswerable, or multi-key item: **fail that item**.
- A benchmark run is only eligible for further educator review at 27/30 or
  higher, with no score of 0 and no critical error. This is an engineering
  threshold, not a validated psychometric standard.
- Release still requires a qualified educator/moderator's signed decision on
  actual exam content, curriculum coverage, memo, and classroom suitability.

Record defect IDs, score, correction, reviewer role, date, and rationale in
`EDUCATOR_RESPONSE.csv`. A blank or unsigned sheet means review is pending.
