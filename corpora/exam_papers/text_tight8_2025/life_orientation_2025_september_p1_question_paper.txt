                               HUMAN RIGHTS VIOLATIONS
                                 Safe and healthy living

 The recent sale of contaminated1 food products by some small businesses in
 communities raised serious concerns about public health, consumer protection and
 human rights. Media reports indicated that some businesses had been selling hazardous
 food products, impacting on safe and healthy living conditions in communities.
 Investigations revealed that many of these businesses were operating illegally, failing to
 comply with food safety regulations, thereby putting the community's health at serious
 risk.

 Although some of these businesses are owned by foreign nationals, the real issue is not
 about nationality, but rather the lack of enforcement of food safety laws and
 non-compliance by certain businesses. However, some of these owners who have been
 accused of selling contaminated products have become victims of xenophobia in their
 communities, impacting on their mental well-being. The Bill of Rights protects individuals
 against discrimination and guarantees the rights of all individuals, regardless of their
 nationality. These public health and safety standards must align with human rights to
 protect consumers from harm. Addressing this crisis requires a collaborative effort from
 individuals, communities and the government.

 Glossary:
 1contaminated: unsafe or harmful for human consumption/use


                                        [Adapted from www.pmg.org.za. Accessed on 2 March 2025.]

 2.3        Explain ONE factor that could increase xenophobic attitudes towards foreign

 2.4        Discuss the possible impact of discrimination on the mental well-being of victims

                        HUMAN FACTORS CAUSING ILL HEALTH
                              Smoking in adolescents

 Factors contributing to ill health can negatively impact an individual's health and future
 opportunities. Recent media reports indicate that there are high incidences of learners
 smoking in schools, probably due to the easy access to cigarettes and vapes1. The
 normalisation of smoking among young people has led to a rise in experimentation and
 addiction, affecting their health and academic performance.

 Social media plays a significant role in impacting the lifestyle choices of the youth today.
 Poor habits and choices made early in life could have lasting effects on the overall
 long-term well-being and quality of life of individuals.

 A focused approach to personal well-being can help individuals make informed choices,
 thereby helping them to avoid harmful substances. Schools play an essential role in
 guiding learners towards positive habits by implementing effective measures. These
 measures could empower learners to make informed decisions that will benefit their long-
 term health and future growth.
