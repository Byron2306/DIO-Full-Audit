Dear colleagues Transparency and ethical conduct are the foundation of our work at the NWU. The 2026 Annual Declaration of Interest review is now open, and your participation is essential to maintaining our compliance with the Higher Education Act. What you need to do Submit your declaration of financial interests, business interests, and fiduciary roles - including those of your family members - via the DIY application on the DIY Services Portal. Your line manager must approve your submission by 31 March 2026. Who must submit This requirement applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees who receive a monthly salary through the NWU payroll. Sessional staff and those paid per claim are exempt. Even if you have no interests to declare, you must still submit a "No interest" declaration. On Behalf of Prof Deon
