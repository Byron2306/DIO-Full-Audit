# HOMS educator moderation pack — Grades 1–12

The workbook has an overview, instructions and one worksheet per grade. It contains 60 exam questions, their matching marking points, question-level CAPS topics, physical PDF page citations, official source links and source hashes.

## Complete the review

For each question, an appropriately qualified subject educator should independently verify grade and term fit against the cited CAPS source, solve the item, check each memo point and its mark, inspect language and accessibility, and record acceptable alternative answers. Use the workbook decision fields and add reviewer name/date. Escalate disagreements or source gaps.

The answer memo and review worksheet are drafts. DIO has completed source-hash and mechanical checks, but no independent subject-moderation decision has been entered. Workbook cells are intentionally blank. Completing the worksheet does not itself authorize classroom release; the institution’s authorized release process still applies.
