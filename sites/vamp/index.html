The route

From agreement to review snapshot.

The service is deliberately bounded. VAMP prepares the evidence trail; the authorised reviewer remains responsible for interpretation and release.

01

Map the task agreement, objective vocabulary, review period and accepted evidence types.

02

Process only the approved evidence set and retain source references and declarations.

03

Map

Separate accepted mappings, review candidates, duplicates and genuine evidence gaps.

04

Approve

An authorised person reviews the snapshot before any delivery or downstream decision.
