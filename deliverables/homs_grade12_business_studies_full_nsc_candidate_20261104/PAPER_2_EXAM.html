Question 1: Case study — Imbali Solar Co-operative (40 marks)

Read the case study and answer all parts.
Imbali Solar Co-operative makes solar lanterns and employs 18 people. Three members want to convert the co-operative into a private company to attract investment. The team has a prototype but has not tested willingness to pay. A supplier offers credit if the business provides security. One founder proposes using a competitor’s product photographs in a crowdfunding campaign. The co-operative has received customer complaints about battery life and is preparing a three-year growth plan.

1.1 Identify two risks in the proposed crowdfunding campaign. (4) [4]

1.2 Explain two differences between a co-operative and a private company that matter to Imbali. (8) [8]

1.3 Recommend two low-cost ways to test customer demand before scaling. (8) [8]

1.4 Discuss how Imbali should respond to the battery-life complaints. (8) [8]

1.5 Advise the members on whether to accept supplier credit, using cash-flow and security considerations. (12) [12]
