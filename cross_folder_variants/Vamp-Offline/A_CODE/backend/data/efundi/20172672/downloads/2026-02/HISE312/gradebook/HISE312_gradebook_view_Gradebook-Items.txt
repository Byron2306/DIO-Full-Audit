Export Gradebook Custom Export Import Selectively import new grades or gradebook items by uploading a spreadsheet (.csv, .xls, and .xlsx formats) below. Note: The formatting of the uploaded spreadsheet must match the conventions detailed below. Conventions used The Gradebook exports grades in CSV format only. For importing grades, CSV is recommended, but XLS, and XLSX files are also supported. The following conventions are used in the Import/Export process: Student ID and Name are the first two columns and must be retained for any future imports. Gradebook Items may include points by wrapping the points in [ ] after the title, e.g. "Assignment 1 [50]". Comments can be imported by prefixing the column with a *, e.g. "* Assignment 1". Columns that cannot be re-imported are prefixed with #. Item score must be a non-negative number with a maximum of 10 digits before and 2 digits after the decimal.
