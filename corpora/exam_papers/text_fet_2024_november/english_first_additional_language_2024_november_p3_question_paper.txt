SECTION B: LONGER TRANSACTIONAL TEXT

QUESTION 2

     Choose ONE of the following topics and write a longer transactional text.
     The body of your response should be between 120 and 150 words in length.
     Write down the NUMBER and the HEADING of the text you have chosen,
      e.g. 2.1 LETTER OF APPLICATION.
     Pay particular attention to format, language and register.
     Spend approximately 40 minutes on this section.

2.1       LETTER OF APPLICATION

          You are a student at a tertiary institution. A local bookshop has advertised a
          part-time position for a shop assistant. You wish to apply for this position.

          Write the letter of application.                                                       [30]

2.2       MAGAZINE ARTICLE

          The editor of a magazine for teenagers is asking for articles on the topic, 'How
          to manage your leisure time effectively'. You would like to submit an article on
          this topic.

          Write the article.                                                                     [30]

2.3       OBITUARY

          The captain of a sports team in your community has recently passed away.
          The club has asked you (as vice-captain) to write an obituary, in which you
          pay tribute to him/her.

          Write the obituary.                                                                    [30]

2.4       DIALOGUE

          You have decided not to spend your holidays with your family. You have met
          with your father to discuss this matter.

          Write the dialogue that took place between you and your father.                        [30]

SECTION C: SHORTER TRANSACTIONAL TEXT

QUESTION 3

     Choose ONE of the following topics and write a short transactional text.
     The body of your response should be between 80 and 100 words in length.
     Write down the NUMBER and the HEADING of the text you have chosen,
      e.g. 3.1 POSTER.
     Spend approximately 30 minutes on this section.

3.1       POSTER

          Your local municipality is hosting a poster competition to promote tourism in
          your hometown. You would like to enter this competition.

          Write the content of the poster.

          NOTE: Do NOT include illustrations or drawings.                                    [20]

3.2       DIARY ENTRIES

          You have recently returned from a holiday that turned out differently to what
          you had expected. Write TWO diary entries. The first entry must express your
          feelings BEFORE the holiday and the second entry must express your
          feelings AFTER the holiday.

          Write the diary entries.                                                           [20]

3.3       INSTRUCTIONS

          You have planned many successful birthday parties recently. Your friend has
          asked you for a set of instructions on how to plan a birthday party for his/her
          sibling.

          Write the instructions.

          NOTE: Do NOT include illustrations or drawings.                                    [20]
