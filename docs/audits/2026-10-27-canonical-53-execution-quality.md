## What was actually inspected

Native customer deliverables were read independently of the outer receipts: review/evidence dossiers, source matrices, examiner/memo text, learner marking feedback, DOCX XML, HTML, campaign copy and media metadata. The baseline inspection read 255 native documents. A separate JSON inspection contains file hashes, excerpts and literal source-statement presence for every product. Literal coverage is diagnostic evidence and does not establish that a claim is true or a professional requirement is satisfied.

The corpus contains small controlled customer-shaped packets. It does not contain full real client manuscripts, complete institutional evidence collections, all 18 publication pages or all 11 figures described in some scenario summaries. Conclusions are bounded to the bytes actually supplied and the engines actually run.

## Variant coverage boundary

The three variants alter generic packet context, fact rows and exception notes. They do not automatically perturb every domain-specific input. These are genuine repeated end-to-end route executions, but they do not prove that those domain engines resisted a malicious instruction in their actual semantic inputs. Document Studio and Sophia source generators include the varied facts; source-profile routes retain the appended note as a review concern.
