The service

A defined evidence transformation.

Evidex does not hide your source material behind a summary. It builds a reviewable line from each claim back to the evidence that supports it.

01

Bound the request

Define the reporting context, the claims to support and the non-sensitive files available.

02

Index the evidence

Record each artifact, source ID, date, type and provenance details.

03

Map and flag

Connect claims to supporting artifacts and make missing or weak evidence visible.

04

Review and deliver

A human checks the pack before the evidence table, claims map and receipt are delivered.

The golden Evidex case

This controlled demonstration begins with an attendance register, field notes, KPI progress data, a photo log and a receipts summary.

The output maps programme claims to those source artifacts and keeps the source IDs visible. It is a demonstration, not a real client or approval claim.
