## Cross-product findings

1. **Mechanical success is not customer quality.** The Stage 7C output establishes deterministic, source-hash-bound fixture execution and refusal behaviour. Every offer remains `commercial_validation=UNPROVED` and `customers_will_pay=UNPROVED` in its own receipt.
2. **The weakest family is regulated or externally consequential work.** Contract Desk, POPIA Readiness, FundingFinder, InvestorProof, and Finance Readiness must not be positioned as decision, approval, or advice products until their authoritative sources and reviewer gates are live.
3. **The most reusable production path is the studio closure.** Finance Readiness Studio, Correspondence Studio, Article Publication Studio, and Site Studio already retain named upstream artifacts. Their Format Core / Document Studio / Lingua / Vesper patterns should replace the generic one-page extension renderer where the product contract permits it.
4. **A real-case gate is missing.** Before any customer-ready claim, each offer needs: customer file hashes; semantic extraction receipt; source sufficiency review; a product-specific expert review; rendered HTML/PDF/browser visual inspection; and signed delivery approval. ProductGrade must stay a separate automated gate.
