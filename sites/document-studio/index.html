Professional boundary

What this is not.

  • Not certified translation. Sworn, legal, immigration and regulatory documents require an authorised provider.
  • Not silent rewriting. Material changes remain visible in the redline and ledger.
  • Not automatic technical authority. Subject-matter facts and safety instructions require owner review.
  • Not proven for every layout. The first lane targets text-readable DOCX, PDF and plain-text material.
