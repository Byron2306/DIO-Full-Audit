# NicheFoundry + LINGUA Commercial Learning

## Doctrine

NicheFoundry does not learn by directly mutating publication, budget or external-action policy from platform metrics.

It learns through the same governed semantic lifecycle DIO already uses for meaning:

```text
held creative draft
    ↓
LINGUA semantic object
    ↓
human-approved publication path
    ↓
Market Command observations
    ↓
DIO attribution: lead → order → verified payment → fulfilment
    ↓
LINGUA commercial learning candidate
    ↓
operator approval for positive reuse
    ↓
BEAST semantic crystal
    ↓
next held NicheFoundry draft
```

There is **no direct learning → execution path**.

## What becomes semantic

Each product × audience × channel draft is registered with semantic units for:

- hook
- buyer pain
- desired outcome
- body
- bounded offer and launch price
- proof angle
- buyer objection and answer
- call to action
- authority / truth boundary

Source hashes and versions make changed commercial meaning visible rather than silently overwriting prior learning.

## What counts as learning evidence

Market Command remains the evidence source of truth.

Platform observations may include impressions, reach, views, clicks, spend and provider-reported conversions.

Commercial evidence may include qualified leads, orders, verified payments and verified revenue through DIO attribution.

Simulated or demo observations are excluded from learning.

A hook is not credited from a campaign-level positive outcome when multiple content items shared the campaign unless the outcome is content-attributed. A single-content campaign may use campaign-level evidence because there is only one plausible creative source.

## Positive learning

A positive or strong-positive outcome creates a LINGUA learning candidate. It does not immediately become reusable truth.

An operator must approve the candidate and its reuse scope:

- `exact`: same product × audience × channel
- `product_channel`: same product × channel across audiences

Approved patterns are draft-reuse only. When BEAST is available they are crystallized as durable semantic credits and recorded on the crystal chain and capability-learning ledger.

## Negative learning

A weak hook does not become a permanent taboo after one disappointing result.

Negative commercial patterns enter an observing state and become active only after three distinct negative observations for the same scoped hook fingerprint. Active negative patterns suppress reuse of that exact learned hook in future held drafts.

This is failure evidence, not a claim that the underlying product or channel can never work.

## Daily operating loop

The user-level marketing service now runs:

1. `build_lingua_hustle_campaign_factory.py --no-reels`
   - observes real market outcomes;
   - resolves approved and active-negative commercial memory;
   - creates commercially scored held drafts;
   - registers every draft back into LINGUA.
2. `render_pending_hustle_reels.py`
   - renders reels only after LINGUA has resolved the draft strategy.

## Authority ceiling

LINGUA learning may:

- rank/reuse an operator-approved hook in the next draft;
- expose commercially promising patterns;
- suppress repeatedly weak learned hooks;
- preserve evidence and lineage for why a pattern was recommended.

LINGUA learning may **not**:

- publish a post or reel;
- activate a campaign;
- spend money;
- create customer authority;
- turn platform conversion value into verified DIO revenue;
- invent urgency, scarcity, testimonials, ROI, certification or professional conclusions.

Publication, spend and consequential external effects remain separately governed operator actions.
