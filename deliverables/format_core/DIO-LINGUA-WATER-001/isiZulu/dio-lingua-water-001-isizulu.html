Inqubo Yokuqapha Ikhwalithi Yamanzi Okuphuza Emphakathini

Teacher Instruction: Inhloso: Le nqubo ichaza ukuthi amaqembu asemkhakheni awaqoqa futhi awarekhoda kanjani amasampula amanzi okuphuza ukuze imiphumela ithembeke futhi ilandeleke endaweni efanele.
Teacher Instruction: Ububanzi: Isebenza kumasampula avamile aqoqwa emadamu, ezindaweni zokukhipha amanzi okwelashwa, nasempompini zomphakathi. Le nqubo ayihlanganisi ukuvuza kwamakhemikhali okuphuthumayo noma ukulinganisa amathuluzi aselabhorethri.
Teacher Instruction: Izemfanelo: Umholi weqembu uhlola ukuthi uchwepheshe ngamunye une-PBT, amabhodlela amasampula ahlanzekile, nemitha elinganisiwe. Ochwepheshe banomthwalo wemfanelo wokuqedela Ifomu Lokulandelelwa Kokutholakala Kwezinto (QMS-07) nokubika izimo ezingaphephile.
Teacher Instruction: Inqubo: Ngaphambi kokuqoqa isampula, vumela impompi igijime imizuzu emi-2. Geza iphrobu ngamanzi ahlanzekile, kodwa ungagezi ibhodlela elinyumba elingenawo amagciwane. Bhala i-pH nokufiphala (turbidity) nge-NTU endaweni. Amasampula kufanele abekwe esiqandisini phakathi kuka-2 no-8 degrees Celsius.
Teacher Instruction: Ukurekhoda idatha: Bhala ikhodi yendawo, usuku, isikhathi, nama-inishiyali zomuntu othatha isampula kubhodlela naku-QMS-07. Ukulungisa amaphutha kufanele kwenziwe ngomugqa owodwa phezu kwephutha; ukusebenzisa uketshezi lokulungisa akukuvunyelwe. Isizathu sanoma yikuphi ukungafundi okungekho kufanele sishiwo.
Teacher Instruction: Ukuphakamisa udaba: Uma i-pH ingaphansi kuka-6.5 noma ingaphezu kuka-9.5, noma ukufiphala kuphakeme kuno-1 NTU, shayela umholi weqembu ngokushesha. Ungatsheli umphakathi ukuthi amanzi aphephile noma awaphephile kuze kube yilapho isikhulu esigunyaziwe sibuyekeze umphumela.
