   2 June 2026 Requesting a review or remark of exam scripts Dear colleague As per the formal communication circulated to all staff and in accordance with A-Rules 1.12.8.1 to 1.12.8.5, the current process for both viewing (review) and remark of examination scripts requires that students submit their requests via the NWU Helpdesk Portal, selecting the "Remark or Review of Exam Scripts" option. The applicable rules are as follows: Paragraph 1.12.8.1 - "Requests for viewing assessment scripts, files or folders pertaining to a formal sit-down examination or assessment in accordance with the traditional assessment practice must be submitted to the faculty administrator concerned via the student portal." Paragraph 1.12.8.2.1 - "... in respect to a module in a contact programme, the application must be submitted within five working days after the module examination results were published following the first examination opportunity, and within two working days following the second examination opportunity." Paragraph 1.12.8.7 - "Remarking of an assessment task at the request of a student is subject to the payment of the applicable fee." (Currently R175.00) Academic staff are kindly requested to communicate the applicable review and remark submission period to students when publishing results and to remind students that requests must be submitted via the NWU Helpdesk Portal. While this process replaces informal or ad hoc arrangements, group viewings remain permissible at the lecturer's discretion. However, to ensure compliance, students must first indicate their intent via the Helpdesk Portal before being included in any such session. To streamline implementation: Front Office staff will issue a standard confirmation reply upon receipt of the request. Once the applicable submission period has closed, a consolidated list of review requests will be provided to the relevant lecturer. Lecturers are requested to include these students in the applicable review process and communicate the relevant arrangements directly to the students. Once the applicable submission period has closed and proof of payment has been validated, a consolidated list of remark requests will be provided to the relevant lecturer for processing. Lecturers are requested to process the remarks and communicate the outcome directly to the students. Academic staff may contact Patricia Thompson should they require assistance or clarification regarding the review and remark process. Thank you for your continued support as we work to streamline and standardise this process. Kind regards Prof Linda du Plessis Senior Deputy Vice-Chancellor: Teaching and Learning
