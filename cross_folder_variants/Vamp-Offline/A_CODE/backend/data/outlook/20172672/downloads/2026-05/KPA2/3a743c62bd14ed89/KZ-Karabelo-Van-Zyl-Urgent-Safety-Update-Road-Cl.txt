Dear Colleagues, Kindly take note of the important update below should you be using these routes for student assessments. Please exercise caution and prioritize your safety at all times. Lichtenburg Road Update – Critical Three main routes are currently affected: R505 (Ottosdal Road): Closed at the Blydeville railway crossing R503 (Coligny Road): Closed with active stone-throwing between Agrico and Toyota R52 (Sannieshof Road): Currently being closed at the railway crossing in Boikhutso Boikhutso is a suburb approximately 2 km south-west of central Lichtenburg on the R52 to Sannieshof. Lichtenburg is currently almost completely locked down. Road Status Overview: South: R505 to Ottosdal – Closed (Blydeville) East: R503 to Coligny – Closed / Stone-throwing reported South-West: R52 to Sannieshof – Being closed (Boikhutso) North: R52 to Mahikeng – Still open (monitor closely) West: N14 to Biesiesvlei – Still open (proceed with caution) If you need to exit Lichtenburg urgently: Use N14 west to Biesiesvlei/Ventersdorp (avoid Blydeville and Boikhutso) Use R52 north to Mahikeng while it remains open Please avoid: R505, R503, and R52 towards Sannieshof Any shortcuts through Blydeville, Boikhutso, or nearby suburbs If you feel threatened, remain in your vehicle with doors locked and windows closed, and contact SAPS (10111). Please share this information with anyone who may still be travelling in the area. Stay safe and remain vigilant.
