 1. 3 IMINQOPHO EVAMILEKO YEKHARIKHYULAMU YESEWULA AFRIKA

 (a) IsiTatimende seKharikhyulamu yeliZweloke samaGreyidi R-12 sethula lokho okungathathwa njengelwazi,
 amakghono namagugu okumele kufundwe eenkolweni zeSewula Afrika. Ikharikhyulamu le inqophe
 ukuqinisekisa bonyana abantwana bazuza bebasebenzise ilwazi namakghono ngeendlela ezizwisekako
 emaphilweni wabo.Ngalokho, ikharikhyulamu ithuthukisa umqondo wokubuthelela ilwazi ebujameni bendawo
 leyo, ngesikhathi atjheja ukukateleleka eembopweni zephasi.

 (b) IsiTatimende seKharikhyulamu yeliZweloke amaGreyidi R-12 sifeza iminqopho yoku:

 • hlomisa abafundi, ngaphandle kokutjheja ibhoduluko lezokuhlalisana-nezomnotho, ubulili, ikghono
 lezomzimba nofana lezengqondo, amakghono namagugu adingekako ukuzanelisa, begodu
 nokuzibandakanya okuzwisisekako emphakathini njengabahlali benarha etjhaphulukileko;

 • ukwenza ifundo yamazinga aphezulu ifinyeleleke;

 • ukwenza kube lula bonyana abafundi basuke emazikweni wezefundo baye emisebenzini; begodu

 • kunikela abaqatjhi ngephrofayili eyaneleko yamakghono womfundi.

 (c) IsiTatimende seKharikhyulamu yeliZweloke samaGreyidi R-12 sisekelwe eenkambisweni ezilandelako:

 • Ukutjhuguluka kwezokuhlalisana : ukuqinisekisa bonyana ukungalingani kezefundo kwesikhathi
 esidlulileko kwalungiswa nokuthi amathuba alinganako wezefundo anikelwe zoke iingaba zesintu.

 • Ukufunda ngomdlandla nangelihlo elihlabako : ukukhuthaza indlela yokufunda ngelihlo elihlabako
 nelinomdlandla, kunokobana kufundwe amaqiniso anikelweko ngehloko ngaphandle kokuwahlaba.

 • Ilwazi namakghono asezingeni eliphakamileko : iimfuneko ezimbalwa zelwazi namakghono okufanele
 zifunyanwe kileyo naleyogreyidi, zibekiwe begodu zibekwe amazingeni aphakamileko, afunyanekako
 kizo zoke iimfundo.

 • Ukuragela phambili : okumumethweko nobujamo baleyo naleyo greyidi kutjengisa ukuragela phambili
 ukusukela kokulula ukuya kokubudisi.

 (d) Amalungelo wobuntu, ukufaka koke, ubulungiswa bezebhoduluko nokuhlalisana : ukufaka imigomo
 yobulungiswa kezokuhlalisana, kezebhoduluko begodu namalungelo wabantu njengombana kuhlathululiwe
 kumThetho-sisekelo weSewula Afrika. IsiTatimende seKharikhyulamu yeliZweloke amaGreyidi 10-12 itjhetjha
 khulu izinto ezifana nokutlhaga, ukungafani, ukungalingani, ubuhlanga, ilimi, iminyaka, abaphila nokukhubazeka
 begodu namanye amatshwayo.

 • Ukukarekela amahlelo welwazi lobugugu bendabuko : ukuthabela umlando onothileko wenarha
 namagugu wenarha le njengezinto eziqakathekileko ukunothisa amagugu amumethwe emThethweni-
 sisekelo ; begodu

 2.1.2.1 Iminqopho evamileko

 Ukuba malimi manengi

 Ukuba malimi manengi kusikhali esiqakathekileko sokuhlanganisa abantu emphakathini, nokuthuthukisa umuntu
 ngamunye nomphakathi wokana. ISewula Afrika iyinarha enabantu abakhuluma amalimi amanengi ngokuba namalimi
 alitjhumi nalinye asemthethweni. Ngalokho, kuqakathekile bonyana abantwana bafunde amalimi wokwengeza
 basesebancani ngendlela okungakghoneka ngakho. Irhubhululo litjengisa bonyana ukufunda amalimi angezelelweko
 kunomphumela omuhle ekwenzeni ingqondo bonyana ihlale imajadu. Amalimi angezelelweko afundeka kuhle
 lokha abantwana banesisekelo esiqinileko seLimi labo leKhaya (LK). Ilimi lekhaya liba sisekelo sokufunda amalimi
 wokwengeza ezingeni lamalimi amanengi.

 Lokha abafundi bathoma bafunda ilimi lokwengeza eGreyidini yoku-1 kungaba sezingeni leLimi lokuThoma
 lokweNgeza (LTN) nofana ezingeni le LBN, kufanele bakhe isisekelo esiqinileko sokukhuluma begodu babone
 okunengi okutloliweko ngamalimi la. Kufanele bezwe ilimi elinengi elilula, elikhulunywako abazokghona ukulizwisisa
 ebujameni lobo. Ukulalela utitjhere acoca begodu afunda iindatjana eencwadini ezikulu ezineenthombe (Iincwadi
 eziKulu) yindlela ehle yokwenza lokhu; kunikela umntwana ithuba lokuthuthukisa ilwazi-magama begodu kusekela
 ilitheresi esathomako ethuthukako. Lokha ukuzwisisa kwabantwana kukhula, bathloga amathuba amanengi
 wokulalela nokukhuluma ilimi ngeendlela ezilula. Bathloga amathuba woku zibandakanyela isizathu samambala,
 isibonelo, emazingeni aphasi lokhu kungenziwa ngokuthi balandele iinlayelo elimini elinqotjhiweko.

 Kuqakathekile ukuhlala ukhumbula bonyana nange abantwana bangakghona ukufunda nokutlola ngeLK,
 bangadlulisela ilwazi namakghono welitheresi alandelako wokufunda nokutlola ngeLTN nangeLBN:

 • ilemuko lefonoloji – ilwazi lokuhlukanisa nokuhlanganisa amagama ngamalungu/ngamasilabhuli namatjhada

 • ilemuko le-othografi/indlela okutlolwa ngayo amagama–ukuzwisisa bonyana amatshwayo asephepheni
 ajamele amatjhada/imidumo

 • ilemuko lamaledere wama-alfabhethi – ukuzwisisa bonyana amaledere ajamele amatjhada /imidumo nokuthi
 amaledere ayahlanganiswa ukwakha amagama nemitjho

 • ukuzwisisa bonyana okutloliweko kuqukethe ihlathululo nokuthi ukufunda kuphathelene nokuthola ihlathululo
 yokutloliweko

 • okhunye okuqakathekileko ngokutloliweko isib. sifunda ukusuka ngesandleni sesincele ukuya ngesandleni
 sokudla

 • imikghwa neendlela zokufunda nokutlola – lokha abafundi banokuzithemba ngokufunda nokutlola ngeLimi
 labo leKhaya bangadlulisela amaqhinga lawo ekufundeni nekutloleni ngeLTN nangeLBN

 • ilwazi lokumumethweko – okumumethweko okutholakele kelinye ilimi kungadluliselwa kelinye ilimi, ngakho-
 ke, lokha ummongo/isihloko esithize sesenziwe ngeLimi leKhaya, lokho kunikela isisekelo sokuthuthukisa
 ilitheresi ngeLTN nangeLBN.

 ELimi lokuThoma lokweNgeza (LTN), abantwana kufanele bakghone ukufunda nokutlola ngokuzethemba
 elimini elinqotjhiweko ekupheleni kweGreyidi yesi-3, khulukhulu lokha nabazolisebenzisa njengeLimi lokuFunda
 nokuFundisa (LFF) eGreyidini yesi-4. ELimi lesiBili lokweNgeza (LBN) lapho kunikelwe khona isikhathi esincani,
 umqopho oqakathekileko kuzakuba ngokukhulunywa ngomlomo.

 Ngokufunda iincwadi ezilula ngeLimi labo lokuThoma lokweNgeza (LTN) nangeLimi lesiBili lokweNgeza (LBN),
 abantwana bazakuthola ukuvezelwa elimini elinqotjhiweko bese bathuthukisa ilwazi-magama labo. Bangasebenzisa
 namakghono wabo amatjha wokutlola abawatholileko ukusekela ukuthuthuka kwelimi labo lokukhuluma (isib. ukutlola
 amagama encwadini yelwazi-magama liqhinga elihle lokuthuthukisa ilwazi-magama).

2.1.2.2 Iminqopho enqophileko

Ukufunda iLimi lesiBili lokweNgeza kufanele kuvumele abafundi bonyana ba:

• thole amakghono welimi athlogekako bonyana bakhulumisane kuhle ngelimi elinqotjhiweko ezingeni elisisekelo;

• lalele, khulume, funde/bukele begodu batlole ilimi ngokuzethemba okukhulako nokulithabela. Amakghono la
 nendlela yokuziphatha kwabantu kwakha isisekelo sokufunda ipilo yoke;

• veze, ngomlomo nangokutlola, begodu nemizwa ukwenzela bonyana batjhaphuluke elimini;

• sebenzise ilimi nemicabango yabo ukuthola okunengi ngabo namasiko ahlukahlukeneko nephasi
 elibazombelezileko. Lokhu kuzaba vumela bonyana baveze ilemuko labo nalokho abakutholileko ngephasi
 labo ngomlomo nangokutlola;

• sebenzise ilimi ukungena/ukufumana nokulawula ukuthintana ebujamweni obuthize. Ilwazi lokutlola nokufunda
 lilikghono eliqakathekileko ‘eminyakeni yelwazi’ begodu lenza isisekelo sokufunda ipilo yoke;

• sebenzise ilimi njengendlela yokuveza ilwazi ngeenhloko ezijayelekileko; ukwenzela ukuhlanganyela
 nemihlobohlobo yamatheksti ajayelekileko; begodu nokufunda umtlolo uwufundela ukuzijabulisa, ukuthola
 ilwazi elisisekelo nokukhulumisana.

2.1.3 Ukufundisa iLimi lesiBili lokweNgeza

Ukwenzela ukufunda iLimi lokweNgeza kuhle, umuntu uthloga bonyana avezwe kanengi kilo ngendlela okungakghoneka
ngayo ukwenzela ukwakha ikghono lephaliswano lelimi begodu nekulyumiswano esisekelo ngephaliswano.Ngakho
ke, abotitjhere kufanele baqinisekise bonya abafundi balalela begodu bafunde iLimi lokweNgeza ngesikhathi esabelwe
iinhloso ezahlukahlukeneko.Abafundi bathloga amathuba wokulalela iLimi lokweNgeza ukuthola ilwazi nokuzwisisa
(isib.Ikulumo pendulwano) begodu nokuzijabulisa (isib.Ingoma/umvumo).Okuqakatheke khulu, bathloga ithuba
lokufunda nokubukela iLimi lokweNgeza ukuthola ilwazi (isib.Iinlayelo zokwenza iimbhedlezwana) ukuzijabulisa
(isib.Indatjana elula).Irhubhululo lisitjengisa bonyana indlela elula yokuthuthukisa ilwazi magama elibanzi kukufunda
amatheksti afaneleko.Kuqakathekile bonyana komlomo, okutlolwako namatheksti abukelwako abe sezingeni
elifaneleko labafundi.Lokha amatheksti abudisi khulu, azabaqeda amandla/phula umoya begodu angekhe bafunde
litho; lokha nakalula khulu, angekhe kube khona iintjhijilo begodu abafundi bazakufunda okuncani khulu.Indinyana
eqakathekileko okufanele idlalwe ngutitjhere welimi, kumetjhisa izinga letheksti nezinga lomfundi.Kisosoke isiGaba
esiPhakathi, abafundi kufanele balalele begodu bafunde amatheksti aselayinini lokuthuthuka kwamakghono wabo
welimi.

 abonompopi, abanganikelwa amagama welimi lokwengeza, isibonelo, iphaphethi uPule. Esifundweni sokuthoma,

 Lokha amalimi afundwa ngokukhulunywa, kufanele kube nokubuyabuyelela okunengi ukusekela ukufubda
 ngehloko. Iingoma zemisikinyeko neenkondlo, ukubuyabuyelela ucoce iindatjana nokufunda ngokwabelana
 kufanele kube misetjenzana eqakathekileko yekharikhyulamu yeLBN. Abantwana bangafindiswa nelimi
 elisayifomula/elizibuyabuyelelako, isibonelo,ukulotjhisana. Enye indlela esebenza kuhle kuSikinya umZimba
 ngokuPheleleko(SZP) okuyindlela yokukhuluma ngokungaphimiseli, isib. abantwana baphendula iinlayelo
 nemiyalo ngendlela emnandi ngokudlala imidlalo efana no ‘Simon says…’. Iingoma zemisikinyeko neenkondlo
 zingezinye iindlela zoku kuSikinya umZimba ngokuPheleleko(SZP). Ukubuyabuyelela uvume sakhorasi ilwazi-

 kufanele kube misetjenzana yangamalanga.

 Enye indlela yokwethula abantwana elimini lokwengeza kulalela iindatjana (nofana amatheksti aliqiniso) afundwa

 lokwengeza (isib. umsana, umntazana). Abafundi kufanele babebawujayele umsebenzi wokuFunda ngoKwabelana

 icocwa nge LK, LTN nange LBN nakukghonekako.

 Enye indlela ehle khulu yokwethula abantwana elimini lokwengeza kukubanikela imiyalo elula bese baphendula

 khudlwana kunekuthomeni. Ilimi langetlasini (isib. Yiza ngaphambili nokuthi hlala emadini) linikela amathuba
 amanengi wokuthi utitjhere athule ukuSikinya umZimba Ngokupheleleko ngendlela yemvelo. Iingoma zemisikinyeko

 ukuzwisisa nokukhumbula ilimi.

 Ubuhle bazo zontathu iindlela ezihlathululwe ngehla (ukulalela iindatjana, UkuFunda ngoKwabelana, noKunyakazisa

 Ekuthomeni, ilimi elikhulunywa bafundi kuzakuba lilimi lokuhlonipha/lokuzithoba – iingoma neenkondlo ezifundwa

 elitjha kufanele lifundwe ngendlela yokuzithabisa ngokubuyabuyelela kanengi njengekhorasi, ukusikinya umzimba
 ngokupheleleko, ukulingisa, ukuvuma iingoma zemisikinyeko neenkondlo ezisekelwe misetjenzana angayibona,
 angayizwa nalokho angakuthinta. Abotitjhere kufanele bafune amathuba wokobana abafundi basebenzise begodu
 batjengise amakghono welimi labo elitjha abasanda ukulithola (ukulotjhisana ngawo woma-3 amalimi, ukuvuma
 iingoma zelanga lamabeletho, izinto zekhonsadi yesikolo, amalanga avulekileko, njll.)

 2.1.9 Ukuphathwa Kwetlasi Neentlabagelo

 Amatlasi weSewula Afrika amihlobohlobo begodu afaka hlangana woke umfundi. Kujayelekile ukuba nabafundi
 abakhuluma amaLimi weKhaya angaphezulu kwelilodwa okungenzeka kungabi liLimi lokuFundisa nokuFunda (LFF)
 lesikolo. Kezinye iinkathi, ilimi elethulwa ezingeni leLimi lesiBili lokweNgeza (LBN) kuyenzeka kube liLimi leKhaya
 labanye abafundi. Eendaweni ezinjalo, utitjhere weLBN kufanele ahlele imisetjenzana yakhe ngendlela yokuthi
 yamukeleke ebafundini abakhuluma iLimi leKhaya nalabo abakhuluma iLBN. Abotitjhere kufanele basebenzise labo
 abakhuluma iLimi leKhaya njengeembonelo, begodu babasekele bonyana badose phambili ngesikhathi ngesikhathi
 sokufundiswa bangani lapho bazakukghona ukusekela abafundi beLBN (abangakhulumi ilimi lelo).

 Kokuthoma, iLimi leKhaya kufanele lisetjenziselwe ukusekela ukufundiswa kweLimi lesiBili lokweNgeza. Amaboda
 wamagama angenziwa ukukhumbuza utitjhere nabantwana ilwazi-magama esele lifundisiwe ngamalimi amathathu
 ahlukahlukeneko. Abotitjhere bakhuthazwa bonyana basebenzise imibalabala ukuhlukanisa ilwazi-magama
 eliqakathekileko leLimi leKhaya, iLimi lokuThoma lokweNgeza neLimi lesiBili lokweNgeza. Imidlalo yelimi efana
 nalo othi (USimon uthi, ukuraya, njll) kufanele isetjenziswe ukufundisa ilimi elizibuyabuyelelako nelwazi-magama
 eliqakathekileko. Iintlabagelo ezifana (nezinto zamambala, amaphostara, Iincwadi eziKulu, Ibuthelelo leendatjana,
 iingoma neenkondlo, amamaski, amaphaphethi okufaka hlangana neeNcwadi eziKulu ezenziwe ngutitjhere
 nabafundi, iincwadi ze zig-zag kufanele zisetjenziswe.
