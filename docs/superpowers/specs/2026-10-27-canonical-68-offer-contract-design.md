# Canonical 68 Offer Contract Design

Every canonical offer must expose a complete customer proposition without inventing commercial proof. The proposition binds the existing product identity, buyer classes, fulfilment route, deliverable families, release gates, pricing hypothesis, and intake requirements into one deterministic contract.

Each contract includes: the buyer and job, a bounded promised outcome, one named primary deliverable with product-specific acceptance criteria, current proof and maturity truth, pricing truth, and a governed intake CTA. An explicit overlay defines these fields for each of the 68 products. Route-family execution proof remains separate from named-product artifact proof. All contracts remain professionally structured hypotheses until external customer validation exists.

Acceptance requires 68 unique product IDs, no blank proposition fields, explicit `UNPROVED` commercial validation, no release or send authority, and deterministic contract hashes.
