# HOMS Assessment Desk Commercial Campaign

**Campaign line:** Assessment work, prepared for educator review.
**Response address:** dio_workflows@outlook.com

## Buyers

- South African teachers with a marking backlog
- Lecturers and tutors handling electronic assessment batches
- Schools and departments that need CAPS-aware papers and memoranda
- Tutors, extra-class providers, and publishers building connected learning material

## Offers

### HOMS Marking Relief

Send the learner work, rubric or memo, and marksheet. Receive structured draft marks, feedback, and an educator review pack.

Pilot price: **R950-R1,800 pilot**

### HOMS Exam Studio

Send the subject, grade, term, scope, and assessment requirements. Receive a draft paper, memorandum, and source or visual pack where the subject requires it.

Pilot price: **R1,800-R3,500 pilot**

### HOMS One-Topic Companion

Define one subject, grade, term, and topic. Receive a connected guide, activity, worksheet, assessment, and memo for subject-expert review.

Pilot price: **Scoped one-topic pilot**

### HOMS Video Lesson Pack

Add a faceless, captioned explainer lesson to the same curriculum spine, with its worksheet and assessment kept in alignment.

Pilot price: **Scoped after one-topic proof**

## Proof We Can Show

- A controlled three-script marking batch produced marks, feedback, a marked gradebook, and a lecturer review summary.
- Exam Studio proof runs produced paired papers and memoranda with CAPS-, grade-, term-, and subject-aware constraints.
- The learning-material and video routes combine existing HOMS curriculum controls with NicheFoundry media production; their release gate is one approved end-to-end topic proof.
- Every output remains subject to educator or subject-expert approval before use.

## Claim Boundaries

- Do not claim that HOMS replaces an educator or subject expert.
- Do not promise automatic correctness, final marks, or classroom-ready use without review.
- Describe generated marks, feedback, papers, sources, and memoranda as drafts or review-ready outputs.
- Do not market learning companions or video lessons as proven until the connected one-topic proof passes subject-expert and media-rights review.
