What she actually does

Not proofreading. Scholarly integrity work.

Sophia combines deterministic audits, source-support analysis and governed model cognition. Every useful claim remains tied to visible evidence, uncertainty and a human decision boundary.

01 · CLAIM BURDEN

What kind of claim are you making?

Causal, comparative, predictive, normative and other material claims carry different evidence burdens. Sophia records the burden before judging the source fit.

02 · EVIDENCE FIT

Does the source carry that claim?

Candidate sources are separated into direct, partial, background or non-support. Broad topical similarity is not silently promoted into evidence.

03 · WARRANT + LIMIT

What connects evidence to your inference?

A visible span can still be insufficient. Sophia surfaces warrant gaps, scope inflation, causal overreach and missing limitations instead of laundering them into confidence.

04 · REFERENCE INTEGRITY

Do citations and references reconcile?

Cited-but-missing, listed-but-uncited, duplicates, incomplete metadata and technical citation problems are separated from substantive evidence questions.

05 · PROVENANCE

What is known, inferred or still unknown?

Sources remain verification leads until inspected. Page locators are never invented. Provider, repair and release provenance travel with the review.

06 · AUTHORSHIP

Does the author still own the intellectual act?

Sophia diagnoses and scaffolds. She does not supply submission-ready replacement prose. Her authorship-preservation signal is explicitly non-forensic and human decisions remain final.

The Speculum

A mirror, not a surrogate author.

Sophia’s deepest object is the Speculum ledger: claim → evidence → warrant → limitation → provenance → next action. It records what the system can see and what it cannot justify.

The example is a controlled product demonstration. It is not a client paper and it is not an accusation of misconduct.

The line Sophia will not cross

No detector cosplay.

No plagiarism verdict from similarity. Similarity can trigger inspection; it does not establish misconduct.
No “AI-written” verdict. The Authorship Preservation Index measures workflow safeguards, not who typed a sentence.
No citation laundering. A DOI, abstract or search hit remains a lead until the source is verified and actually supports the claim.
No ghostwriting. Reviewer commentary diagnoses, prioritizes and scaffolds. Final wording stays with the author.
No institutional authority claim. Supervisors, journals, universities and research offices retain their own policy and misconduct authority.
