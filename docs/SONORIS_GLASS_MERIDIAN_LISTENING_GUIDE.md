# Glass Meridian — listening guide

**Glass Meridian** is a 44-bar, 108 BPM Jazz Synth Fusion prototype planned with embodied gestures before note realization. It is an authored experiment, not yet output from a general-purpose text-to-music engine.

Listen to the normalized [audition MP3](</data/byron/sonoris/rendered/glass-meridian-v002/GLASS_MERIDIAN_AUDITION.mp3>) or inspect the [MIDI arrangement](</data/byron/sonoris/rendered/glass-meridian-v002/GLASS_MERIDIAN_JAZZ_SYNTH_FUSION.mid>). The full [PCM render](</data/byron/sonoris/rendered/glass-meridian-v002/GLASS_MERIDIAN_LISTENING_RENDER.wav>) is retained as the source render. V002 corrects the saxophone response timing found during the first arrangement pass.

| Approx. time | Bars | Gesture | Listen for |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 0:00–0:09 | 1–4 | GLIDE | Electric-piano color and sparse flute establish the space |
| 0:09–0:27 | 5–12 | LOCK | A repeatable bass pocket, ride pulse, displaced piano comping, and the first synth hook |
| 0:27–0:44 | 13–20 | FRACTURE | Syncopated interruptions, chromatic bass exits, drum answers, and a descending counter-motif |
| 0:44–1:02 | 21–28 | ORBIT | The hook is opened into a synth run while the groove continues to hold the floor |
| 1:02–1:11 | 29–32 | HANDOFF | Saxophone takes the foreground; flute and drums thin around the transfer |
| 1:11–1:29 | 33–40 | LIFT | The motif returns higher with denser comping and brighter percussion |
| 1:29–1:38 | 41–44 | RELEASE | The arrangement drops back to sustained color and lets the final sound decay |

The V002 PCM render measures approximately −21.4 LUFS integrated, 7.9 LU loudness range, and −1.5 dBTP.
