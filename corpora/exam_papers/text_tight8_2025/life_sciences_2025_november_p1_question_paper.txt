           birth height, but grow slowly and do not reach the expected height for their age
           group. This condition is NOT due to malnutrition or different lifestyles.

           Scientists investigated the effect of added growth hormone on the height of
           children with ISS.

           The procedure was as follows:

           •       Consent was obtained from the parents of a group of two-year-old boys
                   with ISS.

                   dosage of 0,028 mg per kg of body weight.

                   each participant reached the age of 18 years.
