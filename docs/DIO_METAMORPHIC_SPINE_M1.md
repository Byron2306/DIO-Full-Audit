# DIO Metamorphic Spine M1

## Phase 0 constitution freeze

**Status:** implementation started 2026-08-18  
**Primary law:** **Anything can become anything else except authority.**

M1 is the governed substrate for M2 Commercial Metabolism. M1 is not the terminal objective. The programme is complete only after DIO can both (1) recursively compose and crystallise governed capabilities without widening authority and (2) use settled commercial evidence to improve future semantic projection, offer selection, pricing hypotheses and channel strategy without granting itself SEND, PUBLISH, SPEND, DEPLOY, PURCHASE or other consequential authority.

## Constitutional laws

1. **Metamorphic Law:** a governed unit may change role, packaging, composition and buyer projection without changing its canonical identity or minting authority.
2. **Harvest Law:** before creating a new engine, determine whether the customer job can be solved by composing existing proven capabilities.
3. **Buyer-Language Law:** the customer buys the job; internal organs and proof mechanics remain in the proof tree.
4. **Reality-Binding Law:** validity under world state W0 does not imply validity under W1; consequential compositions bind to a current world lease.
5. **Crystallisation Law:** an outcome becomes reusable settled knowledge only after the appropriate evidence, settlement, quality, transfer/repeat proof, held-out verification and human review where applicable.
6. **No-Authority-From-Learning Law:** semantic similarity, prior success, market performance, crystal reuse, historical approval or composition success may inform eligibility but may never grant or widen present execution authority.
7. **Native-Executor Law:** metamorphic composition must call already-governed native executors and preserve their node-level receipts; a synthetic composition receipt may summarize but may not replace native proof.
8. **Independent-Witness Law:** relevant organs must explicitly report EXERCISED, CORROBORATED, ARMED, NOT_APPLICABLE, MISSING or FAILED. Silence from a required witness is a failure.
9. **Settlement-Before-Crystal Law:** no outcome crystal is promoted before world-state settlement.
10. **M2 Destination Law:** M1 exists to enable M2 Commercial Metabolism safely; market gain/loss/no-response may alter future commercial eligibility and projection only as context-bound evidence, never as authority.
