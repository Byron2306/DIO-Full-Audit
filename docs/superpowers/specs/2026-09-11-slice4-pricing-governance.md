# Slice 4 — Pricing Governance

## Objective

Turn the existing 68-product pricing census from static hypotheses into an evidence-learning pricing system without creating a direct learning-to-authority path.

## Truth model

The governed reference bands remain hypotheses until changed by an explicit operator-approved governance action. Observed commercial outcomes may refine recommendations inside those bands, but may not silently rewrite the band, issue a quote, send an invoice, or create commercial authority.

Only source-bound independent-customer payment plus acceptance may count as positive willingness-to-pay evidence. Self-payment, marker-only payment, refunded/disputed transactions, unverified acceptance, and research-only signals remain non-positive or cautionary evidence.

## Slice 4 flow

68-product governed pricing registry
→ customer-case settlement/acceptance evidence
→ pricing evidence classifier
→ per-product pricing intelligence
→ bounded recommendation
→ next price experiment
→ operator cockpit / Vesper explanation

## Acceptance

1. All 68 products retain explicit governed reference bands.
2. Clean independent paid acceptance is distinguishable from self-payment and contaminated outcomes.
3. Clean evidence can refine a recommendation only inside the current governed band.
4. Out-of-band evidence creates an operator review candidate rather than silently changing the band.
5. No evidence preserves HYPOTHESIS / UNPROVED truth.
6. Every product receives a bounded next experiment with zero send/quote authority.
7. Read surfaces expose evidence counts, recommendation, confidence/truth state, and operator-review requirement.
8. No quote, invoice, payment, contract, or external-effect authority is created.
