   independently checkable requirement and record provenance. Preserve source
   hashes, dates and reviewer identity.
2. Produce and inspect a usable DOCX, HTML and PDF for a normal, messy and
   adversarial case. Check visual layout, domain-specific missing evidence,
   contradicting records, dates, units, and deliberately misleading labels.
3. Record a named qualified human's separate decision on accuracy, completeness,
   professional usefulness and safe scope; retain rejected drafts and corrections.
4. Confirm pricing, scope, turnaround, confidentiality/retention and buyer
   acceptance as separate commercial evidence. No reviewer approval can be
   inferred from internal tests, and no external release is automatic.

Until these conditions are met, the truthful portfolio count remains
