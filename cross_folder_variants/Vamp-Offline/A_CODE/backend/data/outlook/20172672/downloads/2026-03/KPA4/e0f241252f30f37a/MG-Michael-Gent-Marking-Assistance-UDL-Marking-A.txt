Dear Colleagues of SCSSE I hope that I find you well and in good health. The above subject matter refers: Kindly note that any request for marking assistance must first be discussed with the Subject Group Leader/Deputy and the Director/Deputy (Campus) and must be approved in alignment with your individual task agreement. Once approval has been granted and marking has been completed, all relevant forms and supporting documentation must be submitted to the Deputy Director for oversight. The Deputy Director will then forward these to the School Director. Please ensure that all forms are completed accurately and include clear motivation. The “Report To” section must indicate Prof Martha Matashu, as payments must be processed and tracked through the School Director’s account. All UDL marking requests must be submitted to the School with clear motivation in the relevant section. The School will process the payment and subsequently submit a claim to the Faculty. The attached UDL Marking Excel sheet must be completed and submitted to the Subject Group Leader/Deputy and the Director at the start of the semester, for onward submission to the Deputy Dean: Teaching and Learning. In this regard, please complete both first- and second-semester UDL marking assistance requirements and submit them by 30 June 2026, to support budget preparation for 2027. Please consult with your Subject Group Leader/Deputy regarding applicable UDL marking assistance in line with your individual task agreement. Thereafter, the prescribed form must be completed and submitted through the Subject Group to the School Director for final processing of claims via the Deputy Dean: Teaching and Learning Office. The following forms must be used where applicable: P-P-C122F Student Assistant Appointment (TAS): To be completed if the marker is a registered postgraduate student. Undergraduate students may not be appointed as markers. P&C105F Ad Hoc Claim (TAS): To be used if the marking assistant is not an NWU student. ADHOC AND STUDENT ASSISTANT RATES 2026 (003).docx: Please apply the relevant rates accordingly. Please note that the P&C closing date for submission of claims is the 25th of each month for inclusion in the payroll run of the following month. Kindly ensure timely submission before 25 every month.. Should other justified needs that demand for marking assistance arise discuss these with the subject leaders and deputy directors immediately for their consideration.
