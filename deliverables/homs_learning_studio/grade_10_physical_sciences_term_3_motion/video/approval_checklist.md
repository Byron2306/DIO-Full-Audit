# HOMS Learning Video Approval Checklist

- [ ] Subject expert confirms every explanation and worked value.
- [ ] Educator confirms Grade 10 Term 3 pacing and language level.
- [ ] Captions match the narration.
- [ ] Graph labels remain legible at 720p and on a phone.
- [ ] Music attribution ships in the description.
- [ ] Final thumbnail and title accurately describe the lesson.
- [ ] YouTube release remains private until approval is recorded.
