Answer vendor due diligence with evidence, not panic.

What the pilot delivers

Offer

One vendor questionnaire into a response evidence pack with provenance, gaps and reviewer notes.

Source fixture

Generic vendor security/procurement questionnaire + policy excerpts + proof files.

Authority

Automation prepares the work. A human approves claims, release and delivery.

Outputs

  • questionnaire response matrix
  • proof citations
  • gap register
  • review copy
  • delivery receipt

Processors

  • Evidex
  • Sophia
  • VAMP
  • Document Studio

Boundaries

  • client owns final statements
  • no certification claim
  • no unsupported compliance assertion
  • human approval before delivery
