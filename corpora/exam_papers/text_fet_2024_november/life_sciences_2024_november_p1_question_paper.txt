                   CATARACTS – THE MOST COMMON CAUSE OF BLINDNESS

          Cataracts are primarily formed when protein structures in the lens of the eye
          start to disintegrate and clump together. Cataracts is the most common cause
          of blindness and the main cause of vision loss in people over 40 years of age.

          Two types of cataracts that occur are nuclear cataracts and subcapsular
          cataracts. In nuclear cataracts, the cataracts form in the central region of the
          lens and gradually spread outwards. In subcapsular cataracts, the cataracts
          develop at the back of the lens and it is most common in people with
          diabetes.
