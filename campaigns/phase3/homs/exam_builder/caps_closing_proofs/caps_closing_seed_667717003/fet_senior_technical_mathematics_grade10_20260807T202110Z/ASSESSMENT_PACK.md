### Questions

1.1. Simplify the expression: 3(2x - 5) + 4(x + 2) - 7x [3]
   Memo: Step 1: Expand the brackets: 3(2x - 5) = 6x - 15; 4(x + 2) = 4x + 8; Step 2: Rewrite expression: 6x - 15 + 4x + 8 - 7x; Step 3: Combine like terms: (6x + 4x - 7x) + (-15 + 8) = 3x - 7; Step 4: Final answer: 3x - 7
1.2. Solve for x: 2(x - 3) = 5x + 1 [4]
   Memo: Step 1: Expand left side: 2x - 6 = 5x + 1; Step 2: Bring variables to one side: 2x - 5x = 1 + 6; Step 3: Simplify: -3x = 7; Step 4: Divide both sides by -3: x = -7/3; Step 5: Final answer: x = -7/3 or x ≈ -2.33
1.3. A rectangular technical drawing has a length that is 4 cm more than its width. If the perimeter is 36 cm, find the dimensions of the rectangle. [5]
   Memo: Step 1: Let width = x cm, then length = (x + 4) cm; Step 2: Perimeter formula: 2(length + width) = 36; Step 3: Substitute: 2((x + 4) + x) = 36; Step 4: Simplify inside brackets: 2(2x + 4) = 36; Step 5: Expand: 4x + 8 = 36; Step 6: Subtract 8: 4x = 28; Step 7: Divide by 4: x = 7; Step 8: Width = 7 cm, Length = 7 + 4 = 11 cm; Step 9: Final answer: Width = 7 cm, Length = 11 cm
1.4. Solve the quadratic equation: x² - 5x + 6 = 0 by factorisation. [4]
   Memo: Step 1: Identify two numbers that multiply to +6 and add to -5: -2 and -3; Step 2: Factorise: (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0; Step 3: Apply zero-product property: x - 2 = 0 or x - 3 = 0; Step 4: Solve: x = 2 or x = 3; Step 5: Final answer: x = 2 or x = 3
1.5. A technician uses the formula V = IR to calculate voltage. If the current I is 2.5 A and the resistance R is 12 Ω, calculate the voltage V. [2]
   Memo: Step 1: Recall formula: V = I × R; Step 2: Substitute values: V = 2.5 × 12; Step 3: Calculate: V = 30; Step 4: Include unit: V = 30 V; Step 5: Final answer: 30 V

## Rubric

- Correct algebraic manipulation and expansion: 3 marks - Student correctly expands brackets and combines like terms without errors in signs or coefficients.
- Accurate solution of linear equations: 4 marks - Student isolates variable correctly, performs inverse operations accurately, and arrives at the correct solution with proper notation.
- Application of algebra to solve word problems: 5 marks - Student defines variables appropriately, sets up a correct equation based on the problem context, solves it accurately, and interprets the solution in context with correct units.
- Factorisation and solution of quadratic equations: 4 marks - Student correctly identifies factor pairs, factorises the quadratic accurately, and applies the zero-product property to find both roots.
- Substitution and calculation in technical formulae: 2 marks - Student correctly substitutes values into the given formula, performs the calculation accurately, and includes the correct SI unit in the final answer.
