# DIO Phase 14 — Regulated Markets

Phase 14 extends Deterministic Intelligence Orchestration into regulated-market work without converting internal evidence into legal advice, certification, licensing, approval, filing authority, or external release.

## Regulatory Context Envelope

Every run binds jurisdiction, regulator, regulated activity, organisation role, framework version, authoritative-source identity, effective dates, applicability facts, licences, submissions, evidence, retention duties, decision authority, unresolved interpretations, and release state. These dimensions remain independent.

## Regulatory Source Registry

Sources are classified as legislation, regulation, regulator guidance, official form, standard, customer policy, contract, internal interpretation, or historical/superseded material. Authority class, version, effective interval, digest, and supersession links remain visible. Unofficial guidance is never promoted into law.

## Temporal and Applicability Engine

The engine evaluates territorial nexus, regulated activity, organisation type, affected data or people, thresholds, exemptions, transitions, cross-border facts, and evaluation time. Missing or conflicting facts resolve to UNKNOWN, CONTESTED, STALE, REFUSE, or NEEDS_YOU; they are not guessed.

## Regulated Markets Domain

The domain covers privacy, education accreditation, public procurement, and AI regulatory readiness as controlled internal proof products.

## Privacy Governance Framework

PrivacyProof maps supplied privacy obligations, territorial facts, data roles, evidence, and unresolved interpretations. It does not determine legal compliance.

## Education Accreditation Framework

EducationAccreditationProof maps supplied accreditation requirements, institutional role, evidence, review periods, and regulator-facing drafts. It does not award or certify accreditation.

## Public Procurement Framework

PublicProcurementProof maps supplied procurement rules, bidder and authority roles, deadlines, forms, evidence, and submission boundaries. It never submits a bid or regulator filing.

## AI Regulatory Readiness Framework

AIRegReadiness binds the Phase 13 AI Trust Envelope to supplied regulatory sources, applicability facts, readiness evidence, and human authority. Trust evidence is not regulatory approval.

## Regulatory Owner Authority

Consequential interpretation, applicability decisions, licence assertions, regulator submissions, and external release remain human-authorised. Drafting is distinct from filing.

## Regulatory Readiness Dossier Output

Each incarnation emits deterministic JSON, HTML, DOCX, PDF, a proof manifest, and a receipt. Uncertainty, conflicts, stale sources, deadlines, source lineage, AI Trust binding where applicable, and refusal gates remain visible.

## Acceptance Boundary

Phase 14 proves deterministic internal orchestration over controlled regulatory-style fixtures. It claims no live-law completeness, professional opinion, certification, accreditation, licence, regulator acceptance, successful filing, compliance, or external validation.
