   25 May 2026 Reminder: Employee Office Ergonomics Risk Assessment survey Dear colleague As part of the NWU's ongoing commitment to supporting your health, comfort, and wellbeing at work, you are invited to complete the Employee Office Ergonomics Risk Assessment 2026. This short online assessment takes approximately 10-15 minutes and looks at the three key domains of ergonomics: physical, cognitive and organisational. Your responses help us better understand your working environment and identify opportunities to improve comfort and reduce potential ergonomics risks. Please complete the assessment while seated at the workstation where you spend most of your working time: If you work from home 3 or more days per week, complete it at your home office. If you work in the office 3 or more days per week, complete it at your workstation in your office building. All information you provide will be treated confidentially. Results are shared only with the teams directly involved in improving our Ergonomics Programme, and individual responses will not be used for any purpose other than workplace health and safety. Please click here to complete the survey as soon as possible. The survey will close on 30 October 2026. You are welcome to send an email to ergonomix@nwu.ac.za if you have any questions or experience any issues. We value your participation and look forward to receiving your feedback. Kind regards Occupational Health and Safety
