Dear Authors, We regret to inform you that your chapter Playtesting Insights and Self-Directed Improvements in a Serious Game for Teaching Citation Styles, submitted to the SDL AOSIS Book: PLAY-BASED LEARNING TO DEVELOP SELF-DIRECTED LEARNING, could not be accepted. The scholarship of SDL is not of an acceptable standard. Incorrect interpretation of SDL resulted in misrepresentation of SDL which can result in serious risks to the RU SDL and NWU. In this chapter authors used references out of context, used references incorrectly and even referenced themselves incorrectly. Many statements are made without evidence from literature, and it is difficult to follow the argumentation. The authors tend not to have an objective view of their research, and the research methodology is not appropriately explained. It will be a serious risk to the RU SDL and NWU if this research is published. Please refer to the comments from the editors and series editors. This book publication has been withdrawn from AOSIS due to numerous problems with the submitted chapters.
