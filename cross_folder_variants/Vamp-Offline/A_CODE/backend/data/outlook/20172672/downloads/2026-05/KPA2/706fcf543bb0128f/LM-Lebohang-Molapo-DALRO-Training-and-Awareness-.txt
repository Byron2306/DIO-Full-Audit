Click to Join DALRO Session Here Meeting ID: 388 776 996 503 02 Passcode: Ta2Zy7Y7 DALRO Overview: The NWU is committed to complying with Copyright Law and has signed a blanket license agreement with the Dramatic, Artistic and Literary Rights Organisation (DALRO). This agreement allows lecturers to disseminate additional material to students in a timely manner with the stipulation that all material must be retrospectively reported on. Reporting can be done either on DALRO’s Reprographic Reproduction Report Forms or via another method, as long as the necessary details required by DALRO are supplied. The blanket license covers copying on the ‘licensed premises’, being the building or buildings owned or rented by the License Holder (NWU) for educational or research purposes. WHAT IS AUTHORISED IN TERMS OF NWU’S BLANKET LICENSE? • Course packs or readers, i.e., compilation works containing extracts from a variety of published sources, distributed to students; • Single item hand-outs, i.e., journal or magazine articles or extracts from books, that exceeds the reproduction quota determined by the Regulations (Section 13 of the Copyright Act); • Transient (for the duration of the course or module) electronic copies for printing out of substantially identical paper copies, either by the lecturers or students themselves. A typical example of this is the posting of works on any of NWU’s password protected, secure internal networks; • Placement of copies (single items and/ or compilation works) in the library’s short-loan section (i.e. on reserve) for on-copying by students. • Copies in alternative format for visually impaired persons.
