HUMAN FACTORS CAUSING ILL HEALTH Smoking in adolescents Factors contributing to ill health can negatively impact an individual's health and future opportunities. Recent media reports indicate that there are high incidences of learners smoking in schools, probably due to the easy access to cigarettes and vapes 1 . The normalisation of smoking among young people has led to a rise in experimentation and addiction, affecting their health and academic performance. Social media plays a significant role in impacting the lifestyle choices of the youth today. Poor habits and choices made early in life could have lasting effects on the overall long-term well-being and quality of life of individuals. A focused approach to personal well-being can help individuals make informed choices, thereby helping them to avoid harmful substances. Schools play an essential role in guiding learners towards positive habits by implementing effective measures. These measures could empower learners to make informed decisions that will benefit their long- term health and future growth.
