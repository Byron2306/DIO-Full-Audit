# DIO Product Class Readiness

Corrected after full integration review: `2026-08-16`

## Verdict

The previous generated report promoted structural packaging into commercial readiness. That claim is withdrawn.

DIO has 53 atlas product classes, but the evidence must be split into different levels:

- **9 named direct product lanes** have existing engine/commercial proof artifacts and may be treated as controlled-pilot candidates subject to their normal authority gates: Evidex EvidenceOps, HOMS Assess, HOMS Exam, HOMS Learning Studio, Sophia Review, VAMP Performance, Document Studio Edit, Document Studio Localize, and Document Studio Publish.
- **6 capabilities** are operational inside DIO but should not be sold as standalone public products: Market Radar, Opportunity Foundry, Offer Lab, Campaign Lab, Vesper Desk, and Accessible Publish.
- **38 product-class profile extensions** remain registered in the atlas but require a typed executable route and controlled execution receipt before public launch.

The old statement that 47 classes were “ready now” is therefore superseded.

## Evidence Ladder

| Evidence level | What it proves | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- | --- |
| registered profile | DIO knows the product class and its intended composition | executable fulfilment |
| structural proof | profile, intake shape, synthetic demonstration, boundaries and packaging exist | the processor route actually ran |
| execution proof | a controlled typed route ran and left inspectable receipts/artifacts | repeatable commercial demand |
| controlled-pilot ready | execution proof plus reviewed release posture | product-market fit or autonomous authority |
| repeatable commercial proof | multiple paid outcomes and acceptable economics | universal suitability |

A generated page, ZIP, manifest or smoke JSON cannot promote itself from structural proof to execution proof.

## Processing Route Truth

Product-class routing is now governed by `config/product_class_routes.json`.

A route may be one of:

- `direct_route_available`: an existing typed route can accept a qualified lead after its normal source/authority gates.
- `specialist_route_available`: an existing processor can help, but automatic lead promotion is not yet proven.
- `typed_route_required`: a product-class composition is registered, but no executable profile contract is approved yet.
- `route_contract_missing`: do not claim routability.

Qualification is a commercial state. It is **not** permission to process source material, use a remote model, accept service terms, or create authority on the customer's behalf.

## Public Product-Class Surface

The 38 profile-extension leaf pages are intentionally held. The public registry at `sites/product-classes/index.html` shows their evidence status without presenting them as execution-proven products or linking to ignored runtime proof files.

Existing established product surfaces remain the correct buyer-facing routes while the extensions earn execution proof.

## Next Gate

For each of the 38 extensions:

1. approve the typed product profile and source/authority contract;
2. bind it to a concrete processor adapter;
3. run a controlled buyer-shaped fixture;
4. retain execution receipts and reviewed outputs;
5. create a redacted public-safe proof artifact;
6. only then release the product page and controlled-pilot intake route.
