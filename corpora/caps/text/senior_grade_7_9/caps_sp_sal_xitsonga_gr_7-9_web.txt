FOREWORD by the minister

 Our national curriculum is the culmination of our efforts over a period of seventeen
 years to transform the curriculum bequeathed to us by apartheid. From the start of
 democracy we have built our curriculum on the values that inspired our Constitution
 (Act 108 of 1996). The Preamble to the Constitution states that the aims of the
 Constitution are to:

 • heal the divisions of the past and establish a society based on democratic
 values, social justice and fundamental human rights;

 • improve the quality of life of all citizens and free the potential of each person;

 • lay the foundations for a democratic and open society in which government is
 based on the will of the people and every citizen is equally protected by law;
 and

• build a united and democratic South Africa able to take its rightful place as a sovereign state in the family of
 nations.

Education and the curriculum have an important role to play in realising these aims.

In 1997 we introduced outcomes-based education to overcome the curricular divisions of the past, but the experience
of implementation prompted a review in 2000. This led to the first curriculum revision: the Revised National Curriculum
Statement Grades R-9 and the National Curriculum Statement Grades 10-12 (2002).

Ongoing implementation challenges resulted in another review in 2009 and we revised the Revised National
Curriculum Statement (2002) and the National Curriculum Statement Grades 10-12 to produce this document.

From 2012 the two National Curriculum Statements, for Grades R-9 and Grades 10-12 respectively, are combined
in a single document and will simply be known as the National Curriculum Statement Grades R-12. The National
Curriculum Statement for Grades R-12 builds on the previous curriculum but also updates it and aims to provide
clearer specification of what is to be taught and learnt on a term-by-term basis.

The National Curriculum Statement Grades R-12 represents a policy statement for learning and teaching in South
African schools and comprises of the following:

(a) Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statements (CAPS) for all approved subjects listed in this document;

(b) National policy pertaining to the programme and promotion requirements of the National Curriculum Statement
 Grades R-12; and

(c) National Protocol for Assessment Grades R-12.

MRS ANGIE MOTSHEKGA, MP
MINISTER OF BASIC EDUCATION

XIYENGE XA 2: NTIVISO WA TINDZIMI

2.1 Tindzimi eka Xitatimende xa Pholisi ya Kharikhulamu na Makambelelo

Ririmi i xitirhisiwa lexi vanhu va humesaka ha xona miehleketo ya vona no kota ku vulavurisana. I xitirhisiwa nakambe
xa mfuwo na nkhaviso lexi tirhisiwaka exikarhi ka vanhu ku antswisa misava leyi va hanyaka eka yona. Ku dyondza
ku tirhisa ririmi hi mfanelo swi pfuna vadyondzi ku ehleketa na ku kuma vutivi, ku phofula ta xiviri, ta matitwelo na
mavonelo ya vona, ku tihlanganisa na ku burisana na van’wana na ku hlenga vutomi bya vona. Ri tlhela nakambe ri
nyika vadyondzi swifaniso na mianakanyo leyi fuweke, ya matimba na ku enta leyi va yi tirhisaka ku hlenga vutomi
bya vona leswaku byi antswa ku tlula leswi byi nga xiswona na ku va byi vonakala ku tlula leswi byi nga xiswona.
Hi ririmi leri, ku hambanahambana ka mimfuwo na ka vuxaka exikarhi ka vanhu swa kombisiwa na ku vumbiwa,
naswona hi ririmi leri swilo leswi, swi nga cinciwa, swi ndlandlamuxiwa na ku antswisiwa

2.1.1 Swiyimo (tilevhele) swa Tindzimi

Ku dyondzisiwa ka tindzimi eka Tigiredi ta 10 – 12 ku katsa tindzimi hinkwato ta ximfumo ta Afrika-Dzonga, ku nga,
Afrikaans, English IsiNdebele, IsiXhosa, Sepedi, (Sesotho sa Leboa) IsiZulu, Sesotho, Setswana, Siswati, Tshivenda
na Xitsonga - ku katsa na tindzimi tin’wana to ka ti nga ri ta ximfumo. Tindzimi leti ti nga dyondzisiwa eka swiyimo
swo hambana.

Ririmi ra le Kaya i ririmi ro rhanga ku dyondziwa hi vadyondzi. Hambiswiritano, swikolo swo tala swa Afrika-Dzonga
a swi dyondzisi tindzimi ta le kaya ta van’wana va vadyondzi kumbe vadyondzi hinkwavo lava tsariseke eswikolweni,
kambe swikolo swi nga ha va na rin’we kumbe timbirhi tindzimi leti dyondzisiwaka ti ri eka xiyimo xa Ririmi ra le Kaya.
Hikwalaho, mavito: Ririmi ra le Kaya na Ririmi ro Engetela ro Sungula, ya vula swiyimo swa vuswikoti leswi ririmi ri
dyondzisiwaka eka swona, ku nga ri ririmi ra manana (ra le Kaya) kumbe ririmi leri dyondziweke (tanihi le ka tindzimi
to engetela). Hi ku landza xikongomelo xa pholisi leyi, swin’wana na swin’wana leswi khumbaka ririmi ra le kaya swi
fanele ku twisisiwa tanihi xiyimo xa vuswikoti lexi ririmi ri dyondzisiwaka eka xona, ku nga ri ririmi hi roxe.

Xiyimo xa Ririmi ra le Kaya xi nyika vuswikoti bya masungulo byo vulavurisana na vanhu van’wana lebyi lavekaka
etindhawini laha ku nga na vanhu na vuswikoti byo tirhisa ririmi eka swa tidyondzo lebyi nga na nkoka eku dyondzeni
ka tidyondzo hinkwato ta kharikhulamu. Eka xiyimo lexi xa ririmi ntshikelelo wu le ka ku dyondzisa vuswikoti bya ku
yingisela, ku vulavula, ku hlaya na ku tsala. Xiyimo lexi xi nyika nakambe vadyondzi vuswikoti byo twisisa matsalwa,
byo vona vuxongi na byo anakanya leswaku va ta kota ku tivumbela, ku anakanya, na ku ganyisa matwisiselo ya
vona ya misava leyi va hanyaka eka yona. Hambiswiritano, ntshikelelo na ntikelo wa vutshila byo yingisela na byo
vulavula ku suka eka Tigiredi ta 7 ku ya emahlweni i wutsongo loko wu pimanisiwa na vuswikoti byo hlaya no tsala.

Ririmi ro Engetela ro Sungula ri vula ririmi leri nga riki ra manana kambe leri tirhisiwaka ku vulavurisana eka swiyimo
swo karhi erixakeni. Hileswaku ririmi ra ku dyondzisa na ku dyondza eka dyondzo. Kharikhulamu yi nyika nseketelo
lowukulu eka vadyondzi lava va nga ta tirhisa ririmi ro engetela ro sungula tanihi ririmi ro dyondza no dyondzisa. Hi
ku hela ka Giredi ya 9, vadyondzi lava va fanele va kota ku tirhisa Ririmi ra vona ra le Kaya na ririmi ro engetela ro
sungula hi ndlela leyi faneleke na hi vutitshembi hi swikongomelo swo hambanahambana, ku katsa na ku dyondza.

EAfrika-Dzonga, vana vo tala va sungula ku tirhisa ririmi ra vona ro engetela, Xinghezi, tanihi Ririmi ra ku Dyondza
na ku Dyondzisa (RDD).

hlaya na ku tsala kahle hi Xinghezi.

2.1.3 Maendlelo yo dyondzisa ririmi

Maendlelo eku dyondziseni ka ririmi eka xitsariwa lexi hi lama simekiweke eka switshuriwa, lama simekiweke eka ku
dyondza ririmi hi ku ri tirhisa, lama tirhisaka mpfanganiso na madyondziselo yo landzelela magoza.

Maendlelo lama simekiweke eka switshuriwa na lama simekiweke eka ku dyondza ririmi hi ku ri tirhisa ya
lawuriwa hi ku tumbuluxa na ku tirhisa switshuriwa nkarhi na nkarhi.

Maendlelo lama simekiweke eka switshuriwa ya valanga ndlela leyi switshuriwa swi tirhaka hi yona. Xikongomelo
xa maendlelo lama simekiweke eka switshuriwa i ku endla leswaku vadyondzi va va na vutivi bya xiyimo xa le henhla,
va titshemba na ku va vahlayi va vuxoperi, va va vatsari, valangutisi na vatumbuluxi va switshuriwa. Maendlelo
lawa ya katsa ku yingisela, ku hlaya na ku xopaxopa switshuriwa ku endlela ku twisisa ndlela leyi switshuriwa swi
tshuriwaka hakona na nkucetelo wa swona. Maendlelo lawa ya lawuriwa hi ku twisisa ndlela leyi switshuriwa swi
tumbuluxiweke hi yona.

Maendlelo lama simekiweke eka ku dyondzisa ririmi hi ku ri tirhisa ya vula leswaku loko ku dyondziwa ririmi,
mudyondzi u fanele ku nyikiwa vutivi byo anama na ku pfuriwa mahlo swinene loko swi ta eka ririmi leri ri nga ku
tirhisiweni naswona u fanele ku nyikiwa nkarhi wo tala wo titoloveta kumbe ku tirhisa ririmi leri eka swiyimo swa
vutomi leswi hanyaka na ku yelana na vutomi bya masiku hinkwawo. Ku dyondza ririmi swi fanele ku fikisiwa na le
tlilasini laha vadyondzi va nga ta dyondza vuswikoti byo hlaya na ku tsala hi ku hlaya switshuriwa swo tala na ku
tsala hi minkarhi yo tala.

Maendlelo eka ku dyondzisa matsalwa

Madyondziselo ya matsalwa ya fanele ku kongomisa eka ku dyondzisela ku twisisa naswona ya ta katsa naqhinga
ya ku hlaya hi ku landzelela magoza (ku tilulamisela ku hlaya, hi nkarhi wa ku hlaya na le ndzhaku ka ku hlaya)
Xikongomelonkulu xo hlaya matsalwa etlilasini i ku hluvukisa ndzemuko lowo hlawuleka wa matirhiselo ya ririmi
ro hluteka, leri nga na nhlamuselonene, nhlamuselo yo gega, mimfungho na ku enta ku tlula leri va ri hlayaka eka
switshuriwa swin’wana. Hambileswi matsalwa yo tala ya nga yo hungasa, yo hlekisa kumbe ku paluxa swo karhi,
vatsari lava tsalaka hi ku tinyiketa va tsala tinovhele, mintlangu na switlhokovetselo hikuva va lava ku avelana na ku
paluxela vanhu mavonele, miehleketo na timhaka kumbe swiphiqo swo karhi: tani hi milawu, mavonele-xipolitiki na
leswi va swi kholwaka. Matirhiselo ya vona ya ririmi hi ndlela ya vutshila ya engetela eka ku paluxa, ku tiyisisa na ku
tshikelela mavonelo ya vona. Ku dyondzisa matsalwa a swi olovi, kambe a swi endleki handle ka ku va vadyondzi
va nyika matwisiselo ya vona hi ndlela leyi hlutekeke na ku va na ntiyiso. Loko vadyondzi vo ka va nga dyondzi ku
twisisa matsalwa hi voxe, a va nge dyondzi swo tala. Vadyondzisi va fanela ku hunguta mhaka yo nyika matwisiselo
ya nhlamuselo ya switshuriwa na mavonelo ya vona, kambe va pfumelela vadyondzi ku va na xiave lexi amukelekaka
xo nyika matwisiselo ya vona. Ku kuma mongo wa xitshuriwa a hi ku va u kota ku vula leswi nga swona kumbe leswi
nga hoxeka, kambe i ku va muhlayi a kuma mongo wa leswi a swi hlayaka.

Ku dyondzisa loku pfanganisiweke: ndzhendzheleko wo dyondzisa

Hi matirhelo, mpfanganyiso wu pimanyeta ku hambana: ku hambana ka maendlelo, ku hambana ka migingiriko, ku
hambana ka switshuriwa, ku hambana ka minkongomelo (vona Minkongomelo leyi Ringanyetiweke – Nongonoko wa
Swihlovo laha hansi). Loko u tumbuluxa ndzhendzheleko wa mavhiki mambirhi lowu pfanganisiweke, mudyondzisi
a nga veka migingiriko eka nhlokomhaka yin’we, xik. Mintlangu. Ndzandzelelano wa tidyondzontsongo eka
ndzhendzheleko wa mavhiki mambirhi wu nga landzelela nxaxamelo wun’wana na wun’wana, xik. ndzhendzheleko
wu nga sungula hi ku hlaya kutani wu tlulela eka vuencenyeti (ku vulavula) loku ku landzeriwaka hi ku tsala. Xitshuriwa
xin’wana na xin’wana xi nga tirhisiwa eka migingirio leyi humesiweke laha hansi.

Mudyondzisi u fanele a hlawula xitshuriwa xa matsalwa ku va xi dyondziwa. Leswi swi nga va switlhokovetselo,
xirungulwana, kumbe xintlanwana (vona “Switshuriwa leswi tirhisiwaka ku dyondzisa vuswikoti lebyi pfanganyisiweke”).
Mudyondzisi a nga hlawula ku dyondzisa nxaxamelo wun’wana na wun’wana wa switshuriwa. Loko mudyondzisi a
hlawula xitshuriwa xo leha lexi yaka emahlweni xo fana na novhele yo koma kumbe xintlangwana, ku bumabumeriwa
leswaku xi dyondzisiwa hi ku ya emahlweni leswaku vadyondzi va nga rivali vuxokoxoko bya hungu. Loko novhele
yo koma kumbe xintlangwana xi hlawuriwa, mudyondzisi u ta fanela ku avanyisa xitshuriwa hi tiyuniti to hlaya hikuva
ku na nkarhi. Swiyenge swo engetela swa ntirho swi ta hlayiwa tanihi ntirhokaya kumbe tanihi xiphemu xa ku hlaya
ko engetela.

Kharikhulamu leyi yi andlala ntivoririmi lowu wo kongoma lowu nga erivaleni. Hi matirhelo, ririmi ri ta teka kwalomu
ka awara eka mavhiki mambirhi ma’wana na man’wana, hafu ya kona yi dyondzisiwa eka mbangu na ku tsala
na ku hlaya kutani hafu leyin’wana yi dyondzisiwa yi ri yoxe. Ku engetela eka madyondziselo ya ntivoririmi lowu
kunguhatiweke, ku ta va na madyondziselo yo lulamisa kumbe mpfuxeto wa ririmi lowu humaka eka swihoxo leswi
vadyondzi va swi endleke eku tsaleni.

XIYENGE XA 4: MAKAMBELELO EKA RIRIMI RO ENGETELA RA
VUMBIRHI

4.1 MANGHENELO

Makambelelo i maendlelo lama kunguhatiweke ya nkarhi na nkarhi yo kuma, yo hlengeleta na ku hlamusela
vuxokoxoko mayelana na matirhelo ya vadyondzi, hi ku tirhisa tindlela to hambanahambana ta makambelelo. Ya
katsa magoza ya mune: Ku aka na ku hlengeleta vumbhoni bya matirhelo ya mudyondzi, ku hlela vumbhoni lebyi, ku
rhekhoda leswi kumekeke na ku tirhisa vuxokoxoko lebyi ku twisisa mudyondzi na ku ku pfuna eka ndlandlamuko wa
yena leswaku ku ta antswisiwa maendlelo yo dyondza na ku dyondzisa.

Makambelelo ya fanele ku va ya nkamafundza (Ku kambelela ku dyondzisa) na ya mafundza (Ku kambela leswi
dyondziweke). Eka makambelelo himambirhi, ku fanele ku nyikiwa vadyondzi xivikontsundzuxo nkarhi na nkarhi
leswaku ku ta ndlandlamuxiwa ntokoto wo dyondza.

Makambelelo ya vuswikoti bya ririmi ya fanele ku pfanganisiwa. Makambelelo ya xitshuriwa xa xikambelantwisiso
ya fanele ku katsiwa na matirhiselo ya ririmi. Makambelelo ya switsariwa ya fanele ku katsa tinhlokomhaka leti
vulavulaka hi swilo leswi humelelaka evuton’wini.

4.2 MAKAMBELELO YA NKAMAFUNDZA KUMBE YA SIKU NA SIKU

Xikongomelo xa ku kambela hi xikongomelo xo dyondza i ku va nkarhi na nkarhi ku hlengeletiwa vuxokoxoko bya
vuswikoti bya vadyondzi, lebyi nga tirhisiwaka ku antswisa madyondzelo ya vona.

Makambelelo ya nkamafundza i ku xiyisisa ka siku na siku ka matirhelo ya mudyondzi. Leswi swi endliwa hi ku xiyaxiya,
mimbhurisano, ku kombisa hi swiendlo, tinhlengeletano exikarhi ka mudyondzi na mudyondzisi, ku vulavurisana ka
nkamafundza ka le tlilasini, sw. na sw. Makambelelo ya nkamafundza ya nga ha va yo olova tanihi ku yima loko ku ri
karhi ku dyondzisiwa ku xiya vadyondzi kumbe ku burisana na vadyondzi hilaha madyondzelo ya fambaka hakona.

Makambelelo ya nkamafundza ya fanele ya tirhisiwa ku nyika xivikontsundzuxo eka vadyondzi na ku kombisa hilaha
ku dyondzisa ku faneleke ku kunguhatiwa hakona, kambe a ya fanelangi ku rhekhodiwa. A ya fanelangi ya tekiwa
