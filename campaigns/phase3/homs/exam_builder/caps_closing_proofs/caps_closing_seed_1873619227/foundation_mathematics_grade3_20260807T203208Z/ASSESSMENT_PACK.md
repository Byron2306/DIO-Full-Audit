## Concrete Activity: Number Line Jumps

Teacher sets up a large number line on the floor from 0 to 30. Learners take turns jumping forward and backward according to teacher prompts. Each learner completes 3 jumps and records their final position.

### Stimulus

Large floor number line (0-30) with numbered cards, teacher prompt cards (e.g., 'Jump forward 5', 'Jump back 3'), recording sheets for learners.

### Questions

1.1. Start at 0. Jump forward 4, then forward 6. Where do you land? [2]
   Memo: Correct answer: 10; 1 mark for showing jumps on number line or mental calculation; 1 mark for correct final answer
1.2. Start at 20. Jump back 7, then forward 3. Where do you land? [2]
   Memo: Correct answer: 16; 1 mark for showing backward and forward jumps; 1 mark for correct final answer
1.3. Show two different ways to get from 5 to 15 using jumps of 5. [2]
   Memo: Accept: 5 → 10 → 15 (two jumps of 5) or 5 → 15 (one jump of 10, but note: jump size must be 5 per instruction — so only valid is two jumps of 5); Clarify: If jump size fixed at 5, only 5→10→15 is valid. Award 2 marks for correct sequence; If learner shows 5+5+5=15 with three jumps, award 1 mark for effort but note deviation

## Rubric

- Accuracy in number line jumps and final position: 6 marks - Learner correctly performs jumps and records final position with minimal teacher support.
- Ability to show multiple strategies: 4 marks - Learner demonstrates more than one correct method to reach a target number using given jump sizes.
- Recording and communication of mathematical thinking: 4 marks - Learner uses pictures, numbers, or words to clearly show their jump sequence and reasoning.
- Engagement and participation in activity: 6 marks - Learner actively participates, follows instructions, and takes turns appropriately during the concrete activity.
