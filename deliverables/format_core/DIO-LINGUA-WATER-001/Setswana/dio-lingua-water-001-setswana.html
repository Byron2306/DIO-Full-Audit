Mokgwa wa go Lekola Boleng jwa Metsi a Setšhaba

Teacher Instruction: Maikaelelo: Mokgwa o o tlhalosa gore ditlhopha tsa kwa ntle di kgobokanya le go kwala disampole tsa metsi a go nwa jang gore dipholo di nne tse di tshephegang le tse di ka latelwang kwa lefelong le le siameng.
Teacher Instruction: Kakaretso: O dira ka disampole tsa tlwaelo tse di tserweng mo matamong, mo mafelong a go alafa metsi, le mo dipompong tsa setšhaba. Mokgwa o ga o akaretse tshetleho ya dikhemikale tsa tshoganetso kgotsa go lekanyetsa didirisiwa tsa kwa laboratoring.
Teacher Instruction: Maikarabelo: Moeteledipele wa setlhopha o tlhatlhoba gore motekheniki mongwe le mongwe o na le PBT, dibotlelana tsa disampole tse di phepa, le selekanyetsi se se lekanyeditsweng. Batekheniki ba na le maikarabelo a go tlatsa Foromo ya QMS-07 ya Chain of Custody le go bega maemo a a sa bolokehang.
Teacher Instruction: Mokgwa: Pele o tsaya sampole, letla pompo e elele ka metsotso e 2. Tlhatswa probe ka metsi a a phepa, mme o se ka wa tlhatswa botlhelo jo bo sa bolaeng jwa microbiological. Kwala pH le turbidity ka NTU kwa lefelong. Disampole di tshwanetse go bewa mo setsidifatsing magareng ga 2 le 8 degrees Celsius.
Teacher Instruction: Go kwala datha: Kwala khoutu ya lefelo, letlha, nako, le ditšhupiso tsa motlhatlheledi mo botlhelong le mo QMS-07. Diphoso di tshwanetse go suthisiwa ka mola o le mongwe go ralala phoso; ga go letlelelwe go dirisa setlhotlho sa go phimola. Lebaka la go tlhoka go bala lepe le tshwanetse go tlhalosiwa.
Teacher Instruction: Go oketša: Fa pH e le kwa tlase ga 6.5 kgotsa e feta 9.5, kgotsa turbidity e feta 1 NTU, bitsa moeteledipele wa setlhopha ka bonako. O se ka wa bolelela setšhaba gore metsi a bolokehile kgotsa ga a bolokeha go fitlha lekgetho le le letleletsweng le lekotse dipholo.
