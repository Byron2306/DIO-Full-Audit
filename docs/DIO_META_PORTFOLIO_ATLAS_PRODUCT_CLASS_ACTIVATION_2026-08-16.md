## Canonical Meaning Of Registration

A registered class may be used for:

- portfolio reasoning;
- candidate/product hypothesis creation;
- route and profile design;
- synthetic fixture design;
- Market Command planning and held creative drafting;
- evidence-gap analysis;
- prioritisation.

Registration does **not** authorize:

- sensitive source processing;
- remote model processing;
- payment or refund decisions;
- outbound outreach;
- public campaign release;
- final delivery;
- a claim that the typed fulfilment route has executed successfully.

## Route Contract

`config/product_class_routes.json` is now the explicit bridge between atlas names and executable route truth.

A profile-extension entry may name a `suggested_engine`, but `auto_promotable` remains `false` until a typed route has been approved and proved.

This prevents the atlas from silently becoming an execution router merely because two concepts share the same underlying DIO organs.

## Product Doctrine

> META products are compositions over the organism, not new organisms.

A product class should therefore graduate by adding profiles, authority contracts, processor bindings and output contracts over shared infrastructure.

Only a genuinely missing infrastructure capability justifies a new organ.

## Graduation Path

`atlas registration → typed route → structural proof → execution proof → controlled-pilot launch → commercial learning`

The atlas remains the map. Receipts determine how far each class has actually travelled.
