MOOI RIVER CATCHMENT AREA Carletonville Westonaria The Mooi River has two major tributaries, namely Wonderfonteinspruit and Loopspruit. The Donaldson Dam in the upper Wonderfonteinspruit receives water from various sources, such as sewage facilities, mining areas and informal settlements, that has a negative impact on the quality of water. The Mooi River flows south, through agricultural land, from where it eventually joins the Vaal River. The main types of land use in the Mooi River catchment area are crop farming and grazing. According to the Department of Water Affairs (DWA), irrigation and farming are the two major activities polluting the waters of the Mooi River drainage basin, following the removal of natural vegetation. Some small-scale diamond diggings also occur along the Mooi River, destroying the flood plain and riparian (wetlands next to river banks) habitats.
