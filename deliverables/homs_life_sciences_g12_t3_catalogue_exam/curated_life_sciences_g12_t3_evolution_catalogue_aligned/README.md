# Life Sciences Grade 12 Term 3 Curated Exam

This folder contains a curated HOMS exam build for:

- Subject: Life Sciences
- Grade: 12
- Phase: FET
- Term/theme: Term 3, Diversity, Change and Continuity, evolution, natural selection and human evolution
- Assessment type: controlled test with data response, diagram interpretation and investigation design
- Total: 75 marks
- Duration: 1.5 hours

## CAPS And Catalogue Alignment

The build uses the local CAPS profile for Life Sciences Grade 12 and the DBE FET source catalogue at:

`deliverables/exam_source_catalogue_fet_2024_november/FET_EXAM_SOURCE_CATALOGUE.json`

The Life Sciences catalogue contract observed from official papers supports:

- data tables
- graphs/charts
- labelled diagrams/models
- image-heavy source pages

The curated paper therefore uses three source assets:

- Figure 1: finch beak depth data table and graph
- Figure 2: investigation method/data sheet
- Figure 3: hominin skull comparison diagram
