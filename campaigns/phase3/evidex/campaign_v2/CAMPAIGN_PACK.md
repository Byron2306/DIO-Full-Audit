# Evidex Evidence Pack Commercial Campaign

**Campaign line:** From scattered proof to a traceable review pack.
**Response address:** dio_workflows@outlook.com

## Buyers

- NGOs and community organisations preparing donor or programme evidence
- Monitoring and evaluation practitioners consolidating proof
- Grant consultants and small compliance teams preparing review files

## Offers

### Starter Evidence Pack

A small, bounded evidence set organised into a table with mapped claims and provenance for human review.

Pilot price: **R350-R750**

### Standard Evidence Pack

A broader reporting pack with evidence mapping, provenance, gaps, and a clear review trail.

Pilot price: **R950-R1,500**

### Complex Evidence Pack

A larger multi-source pack scoped after intake, with human review before delivery.

Pilot price: **R2,500-R5,000**

## Proof We Can Show

- The golden case converts attendance, field notes, KPI data, a photo log, and receipts into an evidence table and mapped claims.
- Each mapped claim retains a source reference and provenance trail.
- The pack supports review; it does not guarantee donor, auditor, regulator, or funder approval.
