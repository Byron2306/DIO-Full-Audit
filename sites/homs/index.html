The working route

From request to educator decision.

Each pilot has a defined intake, production stage, quality gate, review handoff and revision boundary.

01

Scope

Define marking criteria, or subject, grade, term, curriculum source and assessment form.

02

Produce

Smart Assessor or Exam Studio produces the bounded draft artifacts and evidence trail.

03

Moderate

C8 checks completeness, evidence fidelity, cohort anomalies and phase/term alignment.

04

Approve

The educator or subject expert reviews, changes and explicitly approves before use.

Professional boundary: HOMS supports assessment work. It does not replace educator judgement, institutional moderation, academic-integrity investigation or subject-expert approval.
