## What Is Genuinely Ready

### The shared commercial skeleton

The strongest achievement is not any single AI model. It is the explicit separation of authority:

```text
observe and classify automatically
-> stage reversible work
-> require scope/consent/approval
-> verify payment independently
-> prepare exact output
-> require delivery authority
-> retain receipts
```

The Control Deck, Outlook lease, signed PayPal verification, product review gates, Lingua approvals and Market Command policy all agree on this philosophy. That consistency materially reduces the chance of one agent silently inventing authority.

### Controlled product fulfilment

Evidex, HOMS Marking, HOMS Exam Studio, Sophia, VAMP and Document Studio all have real generated artifacts and state evidence. They are not slide-deck ideas. Evidex has the clearest customer-shaped journey; HOMS has the deepest domain-specific body of work; Lingua/BEAST has the most sophisticated reusable learning and QA layer.

### Acquisition infrastructure

NicheFoundry can produce images, posters, channel copy, reels, long-form proof videos, licensed music receipts and YouTube assets. Hivenance can observe and challenge a campaign hypothesis. Market Command now binds campaigns, governed content, platform reporting, professional roles, publishers and agencies to DIO's own lead/order/payment truth.

### Live infrastructure

At assessment time:

- `101` Python tests and `7` subtests pass.
- All three Market Command Playwright checks pass on desktop and mobile.
- No user services are failed.
- Core commerce, transaction, control, edge and Graph processors are running.
- Daily creative-factory and weekly market-intelligence timers are active.
- The Control Deck reports 2 leads, 2 orders, R950 recorded revenue, 5 active jobs, 8 operator actions and 0 failures.

## What Is Not Ready

### Broad autonomous marketing

Market Command has nine campaigns and eight governed content items, but zero attributed spend, qualified leads or paid orders in its own scoreboard. YouTube is the only live read adapter; Meta, Google, Reddit and TikTok paid reporting still need credentials, while organic channels remain manual. Hivenance therefore has research observations but very little causal commercial feedback.

No system should automatically increase budget from this evidence. The present promotion logic is correct to demand repeated paid conversions.

### External product claims beyond controlled proofs

- Evidex has no external paid closeout.
- HOMS Marking has no educator correction ledger from a real redacted batch.
- HOMS Exam Studio has no external subject-expert sign-off.
- Sophia has no external academic revision cycle.
- VAMP has no external organisation profile/onboarding measurement.
- Document Studio has no independent practitioner validation or paid authorised-document job.

These are commercial evidence gaps, not excuses to rebuild the engines.

### Fully trusted inbound files

Graph attachments are capped, hashed and quarantined, which is good. They still lack malware scanning, content-type verification and a formal trust-promotion receipt before product use. This is the most important technical risk before accepting arbitrary external files.

### Durable operations

The suite is not a Git repository, the root disk is 90% utilised with 47 GB free, and backup/restore and disaster-recovery evidence are absent. The services are running, but the ability to reconstruct the system after a bad edit, disk loss or credential incident is weaker than the application design.

## Final Judgment

DIO's principal risk is no longer that it cannot do the work. It can. The principal risk is mistaking an unusually rich controlled laboratory for a proven market.

At **68.6/100 risk-adjusted readiness**, the right move is neither another rebuild nor a mass launch. It is a disciplined pilot release: one offer, one audience, one tracked campaign, one customer-shaped transaction, one correction ledger, one acknowledged delivery and one honest margin calculation. Repeating that three times would improve this system more than another month of architecture.
