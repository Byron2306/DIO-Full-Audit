# DIO M2 Commercial Metabolism — Phase 8

## Projection adaptation and negative market learning

M2-8 closes the learning side of the commercial metabolism without allowing
commercial evidence to become execution authority.

The verified M2-7 parent contains four source-bound, world-settled and BEAST-
crystallised controlled commercial evidence worlds. M2-8 converts their stable
settled truth into a LINGUA projection-adaptation review candidate and bounded
BEAST learning observations.

## Governing boundary

The M2-7 reference evidence remains `controlled_fixture` evidence. It therefore
cannot establish real market exposure, real payment, independent-customer
acceptance, willingness to pay, commercial validation, repeatability or ROI.

A controlled `GAIN` settlement is useful for proving the structure of the
commercial learning pipeline. It is not positive reusable market truth.

Accordingly:

- all six market claims remain `HELD_UNPROVED`;
- no positive reusable credit is created;
- no semantic market crystal is created in M2-8;
- positive reuse still requires future real source-bound evidence plus operator
  approval;
- learning cannot publish, activate, spend or execute;
- learning cannot mint or widen authority.

## LINGUA projection adaptation

M2-8 reuses `adapters/lingua/lifecycle.py::register_product_source` to register a
versioned commercial projection-adaptation candidate. The candidate binds:

- the exact Funding Proposal Studio commercial context;
- the Phase 2 commercial projection configuration;
- four stable market-truth anchors derived from the M2-7 settlements;
- the six still-unproved market claims;
- the controlled evidence boundary;
- the learning and authority boundaries.

Volatile BEAST block hashes and timestamps are deliberately excluded from the
candidate's semantic identity. They remain evidence receipts, not commercial
meaning.

## Positive evidence

The controlled independent-paid-acceptance scenario reached the structural WTP
gate in M2-6 and a controlled `GAIN` settlement in M2-7. M2-8 records that fact as
a review candidate only.

It does not become reusable positive commercial learning because the current
commercial-learning doctrine excludes simulated/demo evidence from reusable
learning and requires operator approval for positive reuse.

## Negative evidence

M2-8 reuses BEAST's existing `NegativeCapabilityStore` rather than creating a new
negative-learning engine.

Two controlled mixed evidence shapes are admitted as context-bound observing
records:

1. verified-payment-shaped evidence without independent acceptance;
2. independent-acceptance-shaped evidence without verified payment.

Each record is scoped to the exact product, buyer segment, offer, channel and
commercial context. Each has one negative observation, so each remains
`observing`.

BEAST's existing activation law requires three failures before a negative
capability can become active. M2-8 does not fabricate the additional observations
needed to cross that threshold.

A negative commercial observation never globally invalidates Funding Proposal
Studio or its channel.

## BEAST learning ledger

M2-8 reuses the existing `CapabilityLearningLedger` to record evidence-only
lifecycle observations for:

- the projection-adaptation candidate;
- exclusion of controlled gain evidence from positive reuse;
- the two context-bound negative observations.

These are bounded learning records. `direct_learning_to_execution` remains false.

## Exit condition

M2-8 exits only when the LINGUA candidate is source-bound and deterministic, all
market claims remain unproved, controlled evidence is excluded from reusable
positive learning, negative observations remain exact-context and below their
activation threshold, no active suppression is invented, and authority/external
effects remain unchanged.

Acceptance token:

`DIO_M2_PROJECTION_ADAPTATION_READY`
