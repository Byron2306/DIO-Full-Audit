HUMAN RIGHTS VIOLATIONS Safe and healthy living The recent sale of contaminated 1 food products by some small businesses in communities raised serious concerns about public health, consumer protection and human rights. Media reports indicated that some businesses had been selling hazardous food products, impacting on safe and healthy living conditions in communities. Investigations revealed that many of these businesses were operating illegally, failing to comply with food safety regulations, thereby putting the community's health at serious risk. Although some of these businesses are owned by foreign nationals, the real issue is not about nationality, but rather the lack of enforcement of food safety laws and non-compliance by certain businesses. However, some of these owners who have been accused of selling contaminated products have become victims of xenophobia in their communities, impacting on their mental well-being. The Bill of Rights protects individuals against discrimination and guarantees the rights of all individuals, regardless of their nationality. These public health and safety standards must align with human rights to protect consumers from harm. Addressing this crisis requires a collaborative effort from individuals, communities and the government.
